# Lane TR1 pre-registration: an adapter that keeps the nuPlan-board gain without the ego-only speed prior of decision 218

Written 2026-10-10, committed and pushed before any score or probe of a TR1 checkpoint is read (the 20-step trainer parity check and the
arm smokes of `tr1_chain.sh check` are plumbing, not reads). No WA-JEPA or other borrowed weights or features. Logged futures, simulator
state and HD map are labels only. The frozen base model's own lead outputs are inputs. Nothing is submitted anywhere; no on-policy training.
Code: `scripts/pp_train.py` (TR1 block), `lib/parity_adapter.py` (`ego_transform`, `lead_features`), `scripts/tr1.py`, `scripts/tr1_chain.sh`.

Details fixed now: the lead token MLP (7 -> 256 -> 256) is created after every other adapter weight (same init draws as P2 for a seed) and is
pre-trained with its own head on the arm's training rows (target: logged minus base-plan arc length at 0.5 .. 4 s; `tr1.py tok`, with a
speed-only and a no-input control reported). In training the lead numbers come from the teacher outputs (shipped model, same frames); at
serving from a first policy pass of the same checkpoint without the bias (dev ADE is computed through the serving path). The re-timed target
extends the logged path straight beyond its end when the base plan is longer. Anchor rows are unchanged. LG / PO differ from decision 174's

## Stage 0: pilot scale (navsim/op-parity-s234, 3 000 steps x 64, seed 0; the protocol of decisions 197 / 200 / 204)

Six trainings (B0 + 5 arms). Reads, all on navtest tokens unless said (`tr1.py probe` / `report`, definitions = `diag1.py`'s, base model = P0 on the same tokens):
1. **Brake onset**: share of closing-lead frames (base lead head p > 0.5, v0 >= 2 m/s, closing >= 1 m/s, time to contact <= 8 s) with the ego
   not yet braking (fed ax >= -0.3 m/s^2) on which the plan slows (plan acceleration to 1.5 - 2 s <= -0.3 m/s^2). DIAG1: base 0.82, P2H10 0.64 (full scale).
2. **Launch behind a stopped lead**: share of standstill frames (v0 < 0.5) with a stopped lead within 15 m whose plan moves > 0.5 m in 2 s. Reported, not gated.
3. **Acceleration continuation slope**: first-segment speed change per m/s^2, tokens at 2 - 12 m/s, under (i) fed ax +- 2 m/s^2 (DIAG1's probe) and
   (ii) ax +- 2 m/s^2 with the history poses made consistent with it (what a closed loop feeds). The gated number is the larger |slope| of the two.
   Also reported: the observational slope of (arm - base) first-segment speed on the fed ax.
4. navtest EPDMS (`jevdrive.bench`, paired CIs over logs against B0) and WOD val RFS (decision 155 harness, `wod_slot.py report`, against B0 and shipped).

**Gate to stage 1** (all three): onset share >= 0.9 x the base model's on the same frames; slope <= 0.3 x B0's; navtest >= B0 - 0.5.
An arm that misses any line ends (its checkpoint is deleted after the tables are written). Five arms are compared; the gate is a threshold on
each, not a best-of selection, and the pilot read is not a claim.

Registered exception (exploratory, at most one arm): if **no** arm passes all three, and at least one arm passes the onset and slope lines with
navtest >= B0 - 2.0, the one such arm with the highest pilot WOD RFS goes to stage 1 as a *transfer-only* arm. It cannot receive the verdict
"keeps the nuPlan gains"; it exists to put a closed-loop and WOD number on the training-side removal. Below B0 - 2.0, or with a probe line
missed, nothing continues and the lane reports the negative.

Known expectation, written before the read: DIAG1's training-free swaps put every removal of the adapted speed at -2 to -3.3 navtest; the
navtest line can only be met if retraining recovers that through the scene (the plan pathway on vision tokens, or the lead token).

## Stage 1: full scale (navsim/op-parity-full, 10 000 steps x 128, seeds 0 / 1), arms that passed

- navtest and navhard through `jevdrive.bench` against P2H10-F (2 seeds); the stage-0 probes on the full checkpoints against P2H10-F.
- WOD val zero-shot through the existing harness. Line for a transfer claim: RFS >= 8.005 - 0.05 (shipped); reported against P2H10 7.708 and WLG 8.187.
- AlpaSim nuPlan public: the first 700 scenes with OT3's chunk lists, each checkpoint once, recipe = mean of 2 seeds per scene, paired with
  P2H10-F. Line: difference >= 0, log-clustered lower bound > -0.005, at-fault-collision zeros lower. All 1 491 public scenes have been used
  for recipe selection: this is a regression check, not a selection result.
- The winning arm (the passing arm with the best AlpaSim nuPlan difference; a tie goes to the simpler arm) is also trained in the AlpaSim
  input standard with yaw-rate rows (the APY10m10-AB recipe, decision 216) and read on the same 700 scenes against APY10m10-AB.
- AlpaSim PAI: run only if lane PAI2 has made PAI runnable on the GPU box (docs/alpasim.md "PAI on the GPU box", results/pai2_box.md),
  paired with its P2H10 baseline and chunking; otherwise reported as pending with the exact command.
- Simulator stacks: at most 6 for this lane; other lanes' jobs are never cancelled or reordered.

**Verdict per arm** "keeps the nuPlan gains and removes the exported prior": navtest >= P2H10 - 0.3 (2-seed mean), the three stage-0 lines at
full scale, and the AlpaSim nuPlan line. WOD and PAI are reported as the transfer evidence. The report states that five arms were compared.
If no arm clears the lines the report says so; no arm is promoted on a secondary read.

## Plumbing checks (before stage 0)

- 20-step parity: modified `pp_train.py` with every TR1 option off against the unmodified one (worktree of 9c592640), same seed: logged losses,
  dev ADE and the saved weights must be identical. Any stage-1 trainer of the AlpaSim input standard gets the same check before use.
- 20-step smoke of every arm (finite losses, dev eval through the serving path, share of re-timed rows logged).
- Probe identity: B0's `main` plans in the probe file equal its bench navtest plans (same tokens, tolerance 0.03 m on <= 4 s points).

## Budget

About 10 h wall, <= 45 card-hours. Pilot trainings ~6 min each; full ~25 - 30 min each; AlpaSim 700 scenes per checkpoint as measured by OT3 / FIX1.
