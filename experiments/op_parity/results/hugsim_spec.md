## What this says

- Under spec, the shipped model's cost is standing still (decision 118, 119: the model plans a stop); P2's ego inputs remove that
  completely on this set: no max_steps, no launch stall, standing share 0.11. This is the stuck mode WA-JEPA does not have (wajepa_ref:
  max_steps 1 of 64). [I] The fine-tuned plan pathway reads speed through the bias and stops planning a stop; the action pathway was not
  trained, but under spec only its curvature steers and the longitudinal follows the plan, so the plan's speed is what changed.
- The new failure is collisions: a model that now drives where the shipped one stood meets the scenario's actors. Whether that nets out
  positive needs the full 64 (where the 35 scenarios not selected here include the ones shipped Cinque completes).
- The 29 were selected by the shipped model's failure (stuck under spec), so any arm that changes behaviour gains by regression to the
  mean; P1 (fine-tuning alone) gains +0.04 here. The P2 - P1 difference is the input effect: +0.10, CI includes 0.

## Caveats

- Pilot checkpoints (600 steps, 5 400 navtrain rows, seed 0), one run per scenario; per-scenario differences below ~0.2 are not evidence
  (P0 vs stored differs by 0.44 on one stuck scenario).
- The action pathway is the shipped one; the bias reaches it through the hidden tokens, untrained. Under spec it sets the lateral command.
- Subset chosen on the shipped model's outcome (see above); not a sample of the 64.
- Cost: 3 pool jobs (45 GB, 14 cores, 5 workers each), ~20-25 min wall each.
