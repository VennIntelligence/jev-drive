## What it says

- **Fine-tuning on the full navtrain fixes the launch problem and moves HD to near WA-JEPA, but not past it.** P2 and P3 remove the stuck runs (P0 16 / 24 stuck, P2 0.5 / 0) and launch stalls (9 / 18 to 1), and RC goes from 0.33 to 0.51-0.53. P2 is +0.13 over P0 (exam) and +0.10 (spec) with CIs excluding 0, and its gap to WA-JEPA is -0.055 / -0.057 with CIs including 0. P1 (fine-tuned, no extra inputs) gains less (+0.02 / +0.03 over P0) and is still below WA-JEPA with CI excluding 0.
- **P2 > P1 is supported in exam (+0.108, CI excludes 0, both seeds positive) and only weakly in spec (+0.069, CI includes 0).** P3 - P1 is smaller (+0.075 / +0.061). P2 vs P3 is not separable: the seed spread within one arm (P3 exam 0.397 vs 0.327, P2 0.416 vs 0.376) is as large as the P2-P3 gap.
- **The fg-collision count does not grow in a way that the stopped-P0 story explains.** Every arm, WA-JEPA included, has 24-30 fg collisions on the 64; P2 has 28 (exam) / 29 (spec) against P0's 26 / 24. In exam, 17-18 of P2's 28 are in scenarios where P0 collides too (21 / 20 by P0's own end), mostly at step 12-22 in extreme / hard scenarios with an actor at the start; only 9-10 are in P0-stuck scenarios. In spec, 21 of P2's ~29 sit in P0-"stopped" scenarios, but in 13 of those P0 ended in its own fg collision at near-zero speed, so only 7-8 are a P0 that sat out the whole run. The +5 spec fg collisions of P2 over P0 and the much larger bg count (5 to 11) are a mix: P2 drives in the 31 scenarios P0 sat in (9-10 completes, 11-12 fg, 8 bg), which lifts the HD of that set from 0.22 to 0.41-0.44, and it also loses NC / TTC elsewhere (NC 0.81 to 0.68, TTC 0.79 to 0.60 in spec).
- **The remaining gap to WA-JEPA sits in medium scenarios and on kitti360, not in launch.** WA-JEPA keeps an advantage in medium (+0.19 to +0.26 over P2) where it avoids fg collisions that P2 hits; extreme is equal near 0.1 for everyone. P2 beats WA-JEPA in 28 / 24 scenarios (exam), so the near-equal mean is made of large offsetting per-scenario differences, as in the Cinque comparison.
- **Spins.** P1 spins more than P0 in exam (17 vs 9); P2 / P3 are at P0's level (9.5) and WA-JEPA at 4. In spec all fine-tuned arms spin 0-2 times; the exam spins are a controller (PR #57) effect and are not rescued by the checkpoints.

## Caveats

- One run per (arm, scenario). The closed loop is not deterministic across restarts, and the rerun spread is about 0.2 HD per scenario, so per-scenario lists (the > 0.3 lists, the P0 class assignment of single collisions) are noisy; the paired means over 64 are the evidence, and their CIs are bootstrap over scenarios (and scenes) of one realisation, with no run-to-run noise term. Two seeds per arm measure checkpoint spread, not the sim's.
- The P0-stopped class depends on its definition (standing share > 0.5 or v < 0.5 m/s at the collision step); with "P0 ended max_steps" alone the stopped share is much smaller (exam 4 / 28, spec 8 / 30 for P2). Both views are in the tables.
- The foreground-collision breakdown conditions on P0's single run; a P0 that collides at v ~ 0 may be unlucky timing rather than a property of the scenario.
- The 64 scenarios are 45 scenes (clustered CIs shown in tables.md); 16 per cell in the strata tables, so cell CIs are wide.
- WA-JEPA ran with GT route command and GT ego state ([wajepa_ref.md](../../hugsim/results/wajepa_ref.md) caveats); WA-JEPA rows are the same single run for both presets.
- Spin counts for the seed-mean rows are means of two integers. P3-F-s0 spec has one stuck run, P2-F-s1 exam one.
- The navtest readouts of the same checkpoints are in the lane owner's results/full.md and are not used here.
