### 3.1 三把尺子

**R-A 弧长尺（route-conditioned）。** 车道序列 = 日志路径逐点所在的车道（含 connector）按顺序去重得到的序列；navtrain / navtest 用 trajdata MapAPI（`rp_real.py` 的 `lab_log` 同一批元素，d240 / d243 的 M 标签做法），AlpaSim 侧用 `lm.py route_regen` 的 RouteGenerator（MAP）把 `route_start_offset_m` 设为 0 取稠密车道中心折线，或同一 MapAPI 做法，两侧必须同一语义（G-lab 比对一批共同 scene）。
从相机位置在该序列中心线上的**投影点**起量弧长 s ∈ {5, 10, 15, 20, 30} m，取中心点 C(s) 与该处车道沿法向的左右边界点，全部表示为自车后轴系 (x, y)，y 左正。标签 5 × 3 点 × 2 = 30 个数。误差 = 预测点与标签点的欧氏距离（m），逐决策取中位。车离开日志（on-policy 行、零分窗口）时标签仍由日志路径定义，与 d275 一致。它几乎处处有定义，问的是「我的车道往哪走」。

**R-B 近处栅格尺（route-free）。** 自车后轴系 BEV 栅格：x ∈ [0, 24) m，y ∈ [−12, 12) m，0.5 m 格，48 × 48。三类：0 出路（ROAD_AREA 之外）、1 可行驶（ROAD_AREA 之内且非 2）、2 对向（非路口车道，且车道朝向与自车朝向差 > 120°）。路口内一律记 1（connector 方向混杂，不判对向）。读数：三类各自 IoU；**主量是出路类与对向类在 0–20 m 内的 IoU**，分桶：路口内 / 路口前 0–20 m / 20–60 m / 无路口，直行与转弯分开；路口内只报出路类。这把尺子量的正是 S 课训练期用的那张栅格的近处部分（d268 臂 B 的带方向栅格）。

**R-C 视场核查（只算几何，不训练）。** 对三组读数集的转弯决策：R-A 标签点在 s = 10 / 15 / 20 m 的相机方位角，|方位角| > 29.4°（wide 槽外）与 > 15.7°（road 槽外）的比例，按到路口距离分桶；R-B 里出路 + 对向格在 0–20 m 内落在 ±29.4° 之外的比例。它给出 F0 臂的天花板，并**决定第 1 步跑不跑视场臂**（4.1）。

### 3.3 读数、探针与判线（写进预登记 A，push 后才能读任何数）

**探针**：`experiments/corridor/scripts/head1_train.py` 的 `build()`（3 层 encoder + 2 层 query decoder，d 256，3.4 M 参数）原样 import，读冻结 token（8 slot × 32 × 512）+ 20 维 ego（含 command）。
- R-A：`nq = 15, nc = 2`（15 个点的 (x, y)），目标按 P 的均值 / 标准差标准化，Huber δ = 1，缺失屏蔽。
- R-B：`nq = 36, nc = 192`：36 个 query 各管一个 8 × 8 格的块（6 × 6 块铺满 48 × 48），每格 3 类 logit，交叉熵，reshape 回 48 × 48 × 3；多出来的参数只在输出层，< 0.1 M。
- 训练：8 000 step、batch 256、lr 3e-4、wd 0.05、warmup 300，每 500 step 在 val 上取最好；**2 seed，登记读数 = 两 seed 预测的平均**，单 seed 另报。行 = P。
- **blind 底**：同头 `vision=False`（只 ego + command），1 seed。
- **WA-Cf 参照行（只参照，不进方法）**：`experiments/op_probe/scripts/opb_wajepa.py` 的 Cf token（前视、最新 tubelet、4 × 4 池化 → 32 × 512，与 view_39 同形）放进 slot 7、其余 slot 置零，用 `build()` 的 `keep` 只读最后 32 个 token；**同口径配一行「Cinque 只读 slot 7」**，两者才可比。WA-Cf 特征只在真实帧上（J_nav 的 navtrain token 训、navtest-16 读；不碰重建帧与 driver）；缺的约 77 k navtrain token 按 d160 估 20–30 卡分钟。限定写明：WA 的 nuPlan 视频 SSL 是否含 navtest log 未查证（d160）。
- **旧尺子描述行**：d275 的横线尺在同一个 P 上重训一次，复现 rp_real 的 held 转弯 0.90 / navtest 近路口 1.18（G-repro）。

**读法**：三组读数集；按到路口距离分桶（沿日志路径的弧长，`ev.py Geo.junction`：路口内 / 0–10 / 10–20 / 20–40 / 40–60 / 60 m 内无），转弯（`turn40 ≥ 20°`）与直行（< 5°）分开；中位数与 IoU 的 95 % CI 用聚类 bootstrap（navtest 按 log 段，AlpaSim 按 scene），B 2000。

**闸**（全过才读判线）：

- G-ref：WA-Cf 新抽特征在 200 个 navtest token 上复现 d160 的缓存（`representation.md` 的等价检查）。

**2. val and P.** J_nav val = drive logs with sha256("jeval|" + date_vehicle) % 10 == 0. J_on val = the 3 log units first in sha256("jeval|"
+ unit) order among the 28. P = J minus val. All probes of step 0 (and of step 1's read protocol) use this P and this val.

**3. R-A labels.** Logged path = the log's rear-axle poses (navsim: the log pkl at 2 Hz, Hermite-densified as rp_real.densify; AlpaSim: the
scene's rig trajectory). For each token: the logged path from 20 m behind to 80 m ahead of the logged pose nearest in time to t0 (on-policy
rows: the logged pose at the decision's time), sampled every 1 m; each sample is assigned the lane (ROAD_LANE, connectors included) whose
polygon (left edge + reversed right edge) contains it, the one with the smallest heading difference to the path if several (none within
45 deg -> no lane). Route centre line = the projections c_i of the samples on their assigned lane's centre line, in order (this is the
de-duplicated lane sequence, followed through lane changes); boundary points l_i / r_i = where the normal of the centre line at c_i meets that
lane's left / right edge. A gap of more than 4 m of path without a lane ends the sequence. Origin = projection of the camera position (the
token's cam, rear-axle frame -> world) on the route centre line (nearest point); C(s), L(s), R(s) for s in {5, 10, 15, 20, 30} by arc-length
interpolation of c, l, r; in the token's rear-axle frame, y left. A point is defined only if the sequence reaches it; L / R also need a lane
width |l - r| in [1.5, 8.0] m. Both sides run the **same code**; only the logged-path source and the map handle differ (navsim side: trajdata
MapAPI on the after160 cache, world frame, as rp_real.vmap; AlpaSim side: the scene loader's map, as rp.lab_unit). RouteGenerator is not used.

**4. R-B labels.** Cell centres x = 0.25 ... 23.75, y = -11.75 ... 11.75 (rear-axle frame, row = x index, column = y index from y = -12).
Road area = union of ROAD_AREA polygons (holes kept). Junction lane = a lane whose polygon overlaps (area > 1 m^2) another lane polygon
whose centre-line heading at the overlap differs by more than 30 deg. Class per cell: 0 if outside the road area; else 2 if the cell is in no
junction-lane polygon and in a lane polygon whose centre-line heading (nearest segment) differs from the ego heading (t0 anchor yaw) by more
than 120 deg; else 1.

**5. Errors and statistics.** R-A error of a point = Euclidean distance (m). Per decision and arc length s: the median over its defined points
(C, L, R) ("逐决策取中位"); a bucket = the median over decisions. R-B IoU of a class in a bucket = pooled over its decisions (sum of
intersections / sum of unions) in the region x in [0, 20) m (the "0-20 m" main quantity; full 24 m grid reported too); predicted class =
argmax of the seed-averaged softmax. Registered prediction = mean of the two seeds' outputs (R-A: de-standardised points). 95 % CI: cluster
bootstrap, B = 2000, clusters = navsim log segment (navtest) or scene (AlpaSim), percentile. Turn = turn40 >= 20 deg, straight < 5 deg.
Buckets: in junction (jdist = 0) / 0-10 / 10-20 / 20-40 / 40-60 / none within 60 m; "near" = in + 0-10 + 10-20, "far" = 20-40 + 40-60.

**6. Z-0 line, concrete.** Close the eye line ("the frozen eye sees on a fair ruler") iff all of: (a) R-A s = 20 m, turn decisions, near bucket,
registered probe: median < 0.5 m on held-452 **and** on navtest-16; (b) R-B off-road IoU (0-20 m region, turn decisions) in junction / 20-60 m
>= 0.8 on held-452 **and** on navtest-16. Point estimates decide (as the plan's line); the CIs are reported.

**7. Probes.** `head1_train.build()` imported unchanged; inputs = the 8 x 32 x 512 fp16 tokens + the 20-d ego of the token tab, standardised on
the P fit rows. R-A: nq 15 (s-major: C, L, R per s), nc 2, targets standardised on P, Huber delta 1 on defined coordinates. R-B: nq 36,
nc 192 (query j = block (j // 6, j % 6) of 8 x 8 cells, channel = cell x 3 + class), mean cross-entropy over cells. 8 000 steps, batch 256,
AdamW lr 3e-4, wd 0.05, warmup 300 then cosine, grad clip 1.0, bf16 autocast, val loss every 500 steps, best step kept (the rp.head loop).
Seeds 0 and 1 for R-A and R-B; blind (vision=False) seed 0 for R-A and for R-B. One pool job per probe, tags `JEP-<arm>-s<seed>`.

**8. WA-Cf reference and the matched Cinque row (R-A only, reference, not a method).** Rows = J_nav ∩ P (fit) and J_nav ∩ val (step
choice); read = navtest-16 (and all navtest, exploratory). WA-Cf tokens (32 x 512) go to slot 7, slots 0-6 are zero, and the head reads only
the last 32 tokens (`keep = 224 ... 255`); the matched row is the same head on Cinque's slot 7 with the same keep. 2 seeds each, averaged.

**9. R-C.** Azimuth of a label point = atan2(y - cam_y, x - cam_x) in the rear-axle frame (camera axis = vehicle x). Shares outside
29.4 deg / 15.7 deg are over the defined C / L / R points at s = 10, 15, 20, per read set and bucket, turn decisions. R-B: share of the
off-road + oncoming cells with x in [0, 20) whose azimuth from the camera is beyond 29.4 deg. **Rule for 4.1 (registered):** on navtest-16

## Amendment 2 (2026-10-12, before any label value, prediction or read)

trajectory starts at the navsim frame (start token index - 3) with timestamps from 0 and positions equal to the world positions minus that

for all AlpaSim rows, which are clips of navtest drive logs) as the logged path, the trajdata MapAPI after160 map as the map. AlpaSim
anchors and times are moved to it by the offset above (t = timestamp of frame start - 3 + t0). The decision's own logged-path arc s0 is the

(scene loader map + rig trajectory, same code) is what G-lab (iii) compares against on the 20 held scenes, at the navtest token instants
inside each clip. RouteGenerator is not used.

## Amendment 4 (2026-10-12 21:00, after the first label pass, before any probe prediction or read)

The first label pass failed **G-lab (i)**. The share of turn decisions with C, L and R all defined at s = 20 was 0.808 (navtest-16), 0.829
(held-452) and 0.801 (zero-98), against the registered 0.9. Sign (ii) and side agreement (iii) passed: median 0.0001 m on 2 079 points,
R-B cells 100 % equal. The probe jobs queued on these labels were cancelled before any of them started. The only probe outputs that exist
are 60-step smoke runs, used for a code dry run.

Diagnosis: the centre point itself was missing (10 % already at s = 5). Two causes:
- Inside junctions the logged path sat in connector polygons that failed the 45 deg heading test, or cut a corner outside every lane.
  The 4 m gap rule then ended the sequence.
- Switching between overlapping connectors tripped the 6 m jump rule.
