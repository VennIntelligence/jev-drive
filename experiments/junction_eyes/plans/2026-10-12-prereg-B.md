## 3. Read protocol (plan 4.5, made concrete)

- Each arm-seed uses its val-selected checkpoint (plan 4.2). Its trunk re-encodes P and the read sets through the step-1 pixel path:
  - navtrain / navtest t0 pairs from executor 2's `px/` caches;
  - J_on reconstructed slot pairs (amendment 6);
  - held-452 / zero-98 pairs.
- A HEAD1 probe is retrained on P with the step-0 hyperparameters (prereg A amendment 1 item 7). Probe seed = arm seed.
- The arm read = the mean of its two arm-seed probes' predictions.
- **Z' (pipeline-matched comparator):** the factory trunk (LoRA B = 0, depthwise shift 0) through the same pixel path and the same probe
  protocol, 2 seeds. Z' is the comparator for G1 and G2, because step 0's Z read d275's cached tokens and the J_on pixels are
  reconstructions. Z and Z' are both reported. If |Z' − Z| > 0.05 m at s = 15 on navtest-16 near, the difference is reported as a pipeline
  offset, and the lines below are still judged against Z'.
- **Paired difference** Δ = median over decisions of the arm's per-decision error − the same median for Z'. Only decisions defined for both
  enter. 95 % CI: cluster bootstrap, B = 2000, log segments (navtest) / scenes (AlpaSim). The same resample is used for both arms.
- The arm's own joint heads are a descriptive row only.

## 4. Lines

**G1, taught (main line).** Turn decisions, near bucket.
- R-A at s = 15 (F0 arms) or at s = 15 **and** s = 20 (field arms): CI upper bound of Δ < 0 **and** Δ ≤ **−0.10 m**.
- **And** R-B off-road IoU (0-20 m, in-junction bucket), arm − Z' (pooled IoU difference, same bootstrap): CI lower bound > 0.
- Real frames (navtest-16) decide "teachable / close". Reconstructed frames (held-452, F0 arms only) decide entry to the AlpaSim side of
  step 2. The held-452 line uses the same bar, −0.10 m at s = 15.
- For orientation (Z, not Z'): navtest-16 s = 15 near 0.729 → 0.629; s = 20 0.989 → 0.889; held-452 s = 15 1.129 → 1.029.

**G2, not broken.**
- (a) R-A s = 20, straight decisions (all buckets): CI upper bound of Δ < +0.05 m, on navtest-16 (and on held-452 for an arm that enters
  the AlpaSim side).
- (b) E5 on the comma1M held-out 60 segments (`comma1m/junction-eyes-heldout@v1`, prereg A amendment 3):
  - plan ADE at 4 s against the localizer poses, arm / factory ≤ 1.05;
  - action curvature gain A_act within ±0.05 of the factory;
  - read as `experiments/op_wide_ft` / `op_fov` do.
- (c) WOD canary, only for arms that pass G1 and (a)(b): the merged-weights ONNX (plan 5.3) zero-shot on WOD-E2E val. Paired RFS
  difference against the factory 8.005 (decision 183): CI lower bound ≥ −0.20.

**G3, data and field-of-view axes (descriptive; picks the eye for step 2).**
- L-F0 − L-F0-nav on the G1 quantities, real and reconstructed frames.
- L-W90 − L-F0-nav and L-3V − L-F0-nav on the G1 quantities. These are also read on the subset of navtest-16 near-junction turn decisions
  whose R-A label points at s = 15 / 20 lie beyond ±29.4 deg: a decision enters if any of its defined C / L / R points at that s is beyond.

**Closing rule (accepted by the user).** If no arm passes G1 on navtest-16, the eye line closes and no second round is opened. The verdict
is the first that applies:
1. WA-Cf is not better than Z on the G1 quantity: "not in the single front frame". At step 0 WA-Cf is 0.135 m better than Z at s = 15,
   so this verdict would need a reason beyond step 0.
2. R-C says the labels lie mostly outside the field of view, and the field arms did not run or did not pass: "the geometry is not in the
   field of view".
3. Otherwise: "Cinque's eyes are not teachable".

**Into step 2.** At least one arm passes navtest-16 G1 and G2.
- On the AlpaSim side, only an F0 arm that also passes the held-452 G1 qualifies.
- Among several passing arms, pick by the Δ at s = 15. The plan says "s = 20"; s = 15 is used because the F0 arms are judged at s = 15
  only.
- If a field arm and an F0 arm cannot be separated (overlapping CIs of Δ), take the F0 arm.
