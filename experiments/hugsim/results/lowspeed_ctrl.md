## What this says

- [E] A pure controller-side limit at low speed removes the HUGSIM launch spin per event (0 / 96 events against 8 / 91 for the base), with the model, its inputs and its plans untouched. That supports decision 111's reading that the divergence is set by the controller transfer c at launch, and is a second, independent confirmation after the model-side attempts (decisions 96, 100, 101) that did not remove it.
- [E] It is not free: a time constant of 3 s on the steering at < 2.5 m/s slows legitimate low-speed turns and dodges (4 of the 54 non-spinners lose > 0.1 HD, one of them from complete to stuck). The line fails at -0.058 for the lower bound, -0.028 for the mean; the noise floor of the paired comparison is -0.005 [-0.015, +0.003], so the loss is a rule effect.
- [I] The tradeoff is the filter's lack of selectivity: it slows every low-speed steering change, large and small. The spin lean is small (1-2 deg) and the useful turns are large; a selective rule (follow large plan-direction changes at full rate, filter small ones: a deadband or a saturating gain on the plan direction below a few degrees) is the obvious next arm, but it is a new arm with a new pre-registration, not a retune of this one.
- [I] openpilot's own limits cannot be the default for this purpose (row 2): the rule's effective part is a tuned filter with no counterpart in a real car's stack, so on any board it would be a per-board trick (allowed if labelled, user decision 2026-10-04) and must be reported as such.

## Method and limits

- Rule application on HUGSIM: `traj2control` output steering rate scaled by alpha (equivalent to a first-order low-pass of the commanded steering toward the iLQR target), then the curvature-rate clip; the tree is a private copy `HUGSIM-zs/lowspeed` (PR #57 + patch), params in env `LOWSPEED_CTRL`; checked by the smoke (scene-0013: spin -> complete, HD 1.0).
- One run per arm and scenario; the same-day rerun reproduced 8 / 10 of the baseline spinners and added scene-2800_3000, so spin counts have a noise of about +-2 on 10; the non-spin HD lines use the exam baseline as pre-registered and the rerun is the noise control.
- c readouts: synthetic = constant lean in the car frame, plan speed max(v, 1), speed held, a 2 deg lean (c identical at 1 and 5 deg for HUGSIM); logged = regression through the origin of the next-step heading change on the plan's 1 s direction, |direction| < 15 deg, cluster bootstrap over runs; B2D logged c uses the openpilot plan's 1 s point and the truth yaw 5 ticks later, `lat == op`, non-warm, non-zone steps only. The B2D synthetic plant has no steering lag and uses the nominal steering angle (no Ackermann correction), so it is an upper bound; the logged values are lower by 3-6x.
- The offline virtual loops (constant and time-varying gain) did not reproduce the spin runaway (no rule setting changed a sign of z robustly) and were not used to choose parameters; tau0 comes from the c arithmetic above only.
- The B2D filter is a first-order low-pass at 20 Hz (alpha = 0.05 / (tau + 0.05)) on the normalised steer command plus the same curvature clip; the code path is unit-checked only offline (`lowspeed_c_measure.py b2d_syn`), never in CARLA.
