# Why openpilot does not spin at launch on real cars: what comma1M real launches show

Written 2026-10-04. Question (decision 114 point d, decisions 111 / 113): the launch loop gain is high on real camera data (WOD step-1 gain 9.3) and openpilot's own limits give no small controller transfer c (equivalent 0.2-0.5, critical 0.02-0.03), so why do real cars not spin at launch?
Code: `experiments/hugsim/scripts/real_launch_{scan,replay,report}.py`; tables `real_car_launch/{report.md,summary.json,events.json}`. Box run under lease `real-launch` (card 1, released); shipped Cinque, TensorRT, native 20 Hz input.
Tags: **[E]** measured here, **[I]** inference or recalled upstream behaviour.

## What data exists

- **No local real openpilot logs with model outputs, car state or engagement.** The only real-drive data on the box is comma1M (HF `commaai/comma1M`): per one-minute segment two HEVC videos, a fused localizer (pose, velocity, gyro-derived rotation) and a thumbnail. No CAN, no carState, no steering angle, no logged plan, no engaged flag (dataset README: "road-camera video, and full localization data"). Only 8 of the 80 local segments had video. So the fallback of the task was used and the "commanded vs actual" regression of the task could not be done: **[E] engagement and the real controller's command are not observable here.**
- Scan: 600 comma1M segment ids (first by id), 1928x1208 two-camera segments only; launch = |v| < 0.1 m/s for >= 1.8 s, then v >= 1 m/s within 4 s, >= 100 frames of history and >= 2 s after (same onset as the WOD pool: first frame with |v| >= 0.1). **122 launches in 119 segments** (median static before onset 10.2 s, min 1.8 s). Shipped Cinque replayed on the recorded stream at 20 Hz, 160 frames of history, desire 0, traffic flag (1, 0), in two input conditions: `real` (recorded history) and `frozen` (frames before onset replaced by the last static frame, i.e. HUGSIM's static warm-up). Vehicle motion from the localizer (yaw rate, left +; sign check on a first 7 events: corr(phi1, yaw rate) 0.92 over 3 s after onset).

## Answer

**There is no evidence in this data that a small c, or a different history, protects real launches. The real data do not contain a measurable "protective" difference in either factor of the loop; what separates real launches from HUGSIM spin runs in these numbers is the lean at step 1-2 and how fast the car leaves the 0-3 m/s window. The most likely actual reason, that lateral control is often not engaged at launch on real cars, cannot be tested with comma1M and rests on recalled upstream behaviour.**

## Reading

1. **c is not small on real launches [E, with a caveat].** Per 0.25 s step the real heading moves 0.05 / 0.13 / 0.18 deg per degree of the model's plan direction at < 1 / 1-2 / 2-3 m/s, within a factor 1-1.7 of the HUGSIM controller's logged c (0.066 / 0.146 / 0.307) and at 3-10x the critical c (0.018-0.034) for any speed above ~0.5 m/s. Together with decision 113 point 2 (openpilot's limits give 0.2-0.5), the premise "a real car does not spin because its transfer is slow" is now unsupported twice. **Caveat that goes the other way**: these are real drives, not necessarily openpilot-engaged ones. If the driver (or ACC with a human steering) steers, the regression measures "the heading follows where the lane goes, which the model also reads", not a controller tracking the plan; lane geometry correlates both, which inflates c (the first-difference version removes the slow part and gives the same numbers, but a driver who reacts to the same scene still correlates). So this is a statement about the real vehicle + whoever steers, not a measurement of openpilot's closed-loop c.
2. **The model-side gain exists on real comma frames [E].** The realised kernel on actual heading change is 6.1 [4.3, 8.1] at launch steps 1-4 and 1.2 [1.0, 1.4] at steps 5-12; the WOD fake-yaw gain at steps 1-3 is 9.3 / 5.2 / 2.3. Same order, so the HUGSIM gain is not a simulator artefact (consistent with d111) and not a property of WOD's re-rendering either. The product c x gain on real comma launches at steps 1-4 is ~0.3-1.1 (0.05-0.18 x 6), at steps 5-12 about 0.15-0.22 (0.125-0.18 x 1.2), so by this realised estimate the real loop is below 1 per step after the first second, where HUGSIM's was measured at 1.6-2.8 per step with c = 0.19 and gain 5-9 (d100, d111). The realised kernel is a regression slope on partly independent drives, not a perturbation experiment, and it is not the same quantity as the fake-yaw local gain, so this comparison is **[I]**.
3. **The real history is not the difference [E].** Real cars are even quieter than HUGSIM's pretend: yaw-rate std 0.02 deg/s and 0.02 deg of net heading change over 2 s before onset, two orders below the +-2 deg/s probe. Replacing the recorded history by the HUGSIM-style frozen frame changes the step 1-2 lean by a median 0.009 deg (CI -0.05 to +0.02); the mean lean is slightly smaller with the real history (-0.25 [-0.58, -0.04]), which is the heavy step-1 tail of the frozen version (mean 1.73 vs 0.89), i.e. a jump from a perfectly frozen frame to the first moving frame, a HUGSIM-warm-up artefact that real video does not have, but it is the 5-10 % tail, not the median. Creep, camera vibration and yaw noise do not appear as a systematic history difference at the model's 20 Hz input. (The plan direction while standing still is unusable: median 7 deg and real-vs-frozen differing by 28 deg rms, because the 0.8 s plan point is a few centimetres from the origin; no conclusion is drawn from the static phase. Vibration above 10 Hz, i.e. above the 20 Hz Nyquist of the localizer, is not measured.)
4. **Lean: real is on the small side [E].** Median 0.39 deg at steps 1-2 against 0.43 (HUGSIM non-spin), 0.76 (HUGSIM spin), 0.58 (WOD); 22% >= 1 deg and 13% >= 2 deg per event, WOD 39% >= 1 deg. Real launches are therefore not an easier-than-HUGSIM population in the first step by much, but the strong-lean tail is lighter. Events with lean >= 2 deg (16) have a 3 s heading change median 6.3 deg (p90 39) and 38% above 10 deg, against 0.35 deg median for lean < 1 deg (n 84): a strong early lean goes with a real heading change, as in HUGSIM. That cannot separate a loop from a real turn (the lean is partly the road).
5. **Dwell in the 0-3 m/s window [E for the speeds, I for the role].** Real launches accelerate to 3.4 m/s in 2 s (median) and are out of the window after ~1.8 s; HUGSIM base runs are at 2.1-2.6 m/s at 2-3 s and spend a median 6.4 s of the first 10 s below 3 m/s (spin runs 1.1 / 2.1 / 3.6 m/s at 1 / 2 / 3 s). A loop with growth ~2 per step and gain decaying from 6 to 1.2 over the first five steps has about 4-6 steps to grow in the real case and 10+ in HUGSIM. At the same time the real car's 0.4 deg at step 4 does not grow (0.07 deg at +1 s, 0.26 at +2 s), where HUGSIM spins go 0.4 -> 10 deg in four steps. This is consistent with "less time in the sensitive regime", not proven by it: the two accelerations differ because HUGSIM's controller/vehicle accelerates slower, an effect of the simulator's longitudinal side.
6. **Engagement [not testable here; I].** comma1M has no engagement flag. From recalled upstream behaviour (no vendored source, treat as unverified): openpilot lateral control is only active above a minimum speed (the controls layer uses a small non-zero floor, and most cars have a car-specific `minSteerSpeed`, several above 3 m/s, some above 10 m/s); torque cars additionally ramp the steering command up. If so, launches on real cars are mostly under driver steering for the first seconds, and the loop that spins HUGSIM simply is not closed at 0-3 m/s on most of the fleet. This alone would explain the absence of spins, and it is consistent with every number above (the heading response c that we measure is then the driver's, which tracks the same road the model reads). To check it, one needs openpilot rlogs with `selfdriveState.active` / `carControl.latActive` (comma2k19, the connect API or the lab's own drives); none are on the box.

## Best-supported explanation

Ranked by support:

1. **[I, not testable here] Lateral is not (or only weakly) engaged at the first seconds of real launches.** Everything else is consistent with it, nothing here contradicts it, and the other candidates below do not show up in the real data.
2. **[E partly, I for the role] Shorter dwell in the sensitive window plus a decaying gain**: real launches leave 0-3 m/s in ~1.8 s with the realised kernel falling from 6 to 1.2, HUGSIM spends several seconds there. Not a property of the controller; a property of the longitudinal profile and of how long the loop acts.
3. **[E] Not supported**: small c (0.05-0.18 measured, 3-10x critical), noisy / different history (real history quieter than HUGSIM's probe, no effect on the lean), a lower model gain on real frames (6 realised, 9.3 WOD).
4. Lean at launch is only slightly smaller on real drives (0.39 vs 0.43-0.76 median): it does not by itself explain a difference.

**What this means for the spin work (decisions 113/114)**: the rule-side arms (low-pass, selective dead zone) have no counterpart in a real stack, and the real-data evidence does not point to a small-c controller either; they remain per-board tricks. The most informative single next datum is an openpilot rlog with engagement flags at launch; if a launch lane is ever engaged at 0-3 m/s on real cars, its logged `desiredCurvature` vs the plan gives the c that this note could not measure.

## Limits

- n = 122 launches from the first 600 comma1M ids with 1928x1208 cameras (comma 3 / 3X); fleet mix, vehicles and drivers unknown; the localizer yaw is a fused estimate at 20 Hz. The speed of a "static" frame has a noise floor of ~0.05 m/s (threshold 0.1).
- phi1 is HUGSIM's 1 s point at DIL 1.25 (0.8 s model time), read on the real 20 Hz stream with no desire (0) and traffic flag (1, 0), left-hand / right-hand not tested. The model clock is real, so the dilation only fixes the readout point.
- c was read per 0.25 s step starting at the onset with |phi1| < 15 deg; steps are not independent within an event (cluster CI over events). A launch is included whether or not openpilot was engaged.
- The yaw-rate sign check is in the first run on 7 events (0.92); the full-run line printed `nan` (a length filter in the check, not in any reported number).
- The comparison numbers (HUGSIM, WOD, B2D) are taken from decisions 100/110/111/113 and `lowspeed_ctrl.md` as reported; the HUGSIM speed profile was recomputed from the `cinque-fixed-base` zs_steps logs (all 64 runs; the spin subset match was incomplete, 3 of 10).
