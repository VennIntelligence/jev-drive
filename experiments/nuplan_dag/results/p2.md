## Reading

The teacher is geometrically sound on nuPlan (T1, T2, and the BEV plots: B2 swings up to 3 m wide in right turns and the teacher pulls it back). Even so, 4 shape-labelled rows per batch did not reduce the arena lateral zeros. Neither the over-speed class (a) nor the path class (b) went down; both drifted up slightly, within about one seed's spread.

This matches decision 395. The arena lateral zeros are over-speed failures, and a label that keeps the student's own served arc length does not slow it down. The k = 9 speed ratio rose by 0.01 / 0.03. One plausible cause is that imitating the student's own fast arcs reinforces its pace; that reading is mechanism-level and untested.

Held 460 (longer windows) improved on zeros and lateral zeros in both seeds, but this was not the registered adoption read.

**Not adopted.** B2 stays the nuPlan candidate. A nuPlan lateral teacher needs the longitudinal part (TURN-CAP / an arc cap in sharp turns) to act on these zeros.
