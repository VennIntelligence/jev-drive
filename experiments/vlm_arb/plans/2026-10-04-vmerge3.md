## 1. The bypass without privileged input (VM3_BYP=perc)

| input | vmerge2 (pbyp3, privileged) | vmerge3 |
|:--|:--|:--|
| trigger | stood >= 5 s + openpilot lead head (p > 0.5, x < 40 m, v < 1 m/s); no VLM red within 5 s; > 15 m from a route junction | unchanged |
| obstacle exists | simulator actors standing >= 5 s on the route | the lead head itself (and |lead y| <= 1.5 m) |
| obstacle start | actor bounding boxes | ego arc + camera offset + lead x; moved closer while the lead head sees it and the car has not committed |
| obstacle extent | actor bounding boxes | prior 15 m past the lead (one parked car ~5 m, construction / accident sites up to ~15 m in Bench2Drive), extended by static front-radar returns in the ego's original lane band that are contiguous (gaps <= 8 m), at most 40 m past the start |
| adjacent lane, side, offset | CARLA map lanes (`get_left_lane` / `get_right_lane`, lane type, direction) | openpilot lane lines and road edges (median over 8-30 m ahead): ego lane width w = right line - left line (accepted 2.6-4.5 m); a side has a lane if its road edge is >= 2.6 m beyond the ego lane line; offset = w toward that side; both sides: the right one (right-hand traffic: the right lane runs the ego's way); neither: no bypass |
| direction / gap | simulator velocities in the target lane: same-direction vehicles alongside or within 2 m + 0.5 s x closing | radars only: two rear-corner radars (blind-spot monitoring, the BSM input of openpilot's lane-change assist) and a front radar (Bench2Drive allows 4). Target-lane band = route lateral within 1.6 m of the offset. Before the pull-out began (shift < 5 %): refuse while a moving return closes from behind within 3 m + closing speed x 2.5 s, or was seen within 8 m behind the rear bumper in the last 1.0 s (it may be alongside); after it began (5-30 % of the shift): only within 1.5 m + closing x 0.8 s; front radar: refuse while an approaching return in the band is within 15 m or arrives before the manoeuvre ends (vmerge2's oncoming rule). Both checks apply on either side, so the lane direction is not needed |
| path, commit, tighter start | route shifted by the offset with smooth ramps, committed at 30 % of the shift | unchanged |

The gap rule changed from pbyp3 on purpose: pullout_collisions.md says pull-outs ended in contact when the car started alongside or in a
2 s gap at walking speed, and that the follower yields to a car that committed ahead of it. With a median headway of 3.2 s, a start rule of
2.5 s fires just after a vehicle has passed (the next one is about 3 s back); the after-start rule is the alongside test only, so the car does not
swerve back for a vehicle that is still far.

Not used: the optional Qwen "adjacent lane free / same direction" question (the radars cover both directions); the CARLA map in any form.
Privileged geometry keeps running in arm "drive" (logs only: obstacles, actors, contacts), so the perception can be scored against it.

## 2. Release check (VM3_REL=1)

Question `/cross` on the wide + road frame, two options (`vehicle_crossing` / `path_clear`), one forward pass, the same server (one per
card slot). Prompt and threshold chosen offline before this batch on the saved frames of the vmerge-family runs
(`results/vm3_cross_offline.md`, its own pre-registered line: test half, junction stops, recall >= 0.6 and FPR <= 0.15). Rules:

- asked at 2 Hz (0.25 s after a light slot) while R2 holds with cusum >= 2.5, while R3 stands at its line, while a release waits, and for 4 s after a release;
- an R2 cusum release (cusum >= 5) and the end of an R3 dwell go ahead only when the last 2 cross answers are fresh (first one <= 2.1 s old)
  and both have P(vehicle_crossing) < thr (thr from the offline step; 0.5 if it gives none); otherwise the hold stays, at most 8 s
  (then released, counted as a timeout);
- R6: within 4 s after a release, two fresh crossing answers stop the car again at the same line if it can still stop before it (and
  v < 5 m/s); released like a pending release.

What it cannot fix and is not claimed: 17280's hits happen while the car stands at the stop sign before any release.
