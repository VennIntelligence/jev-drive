2026-10-02. Follows [2026-10-02-vlm-arb.md](2026-10-02-vlm-arb.md) (registered table, parameters, lines, deviations D1-D16) and
[2026-10-02-vlm-thin.md](2026-10-02-vlm-thin.md) (the fast reading). Nothing registered there is changed: every threshold, K, TTL,

`lib/vlm_arb_agent.py` (backend switch).

## 1. Question

Does a zero-shot VLM reading the traffic light, wired into the existing arbitration layer, reduce red-light infractions in
Bench2Drive (CARLA)? Arm `vred` = `drive` + row R2 (red light: stop before the junction entrance on K = 2 consecutive
`red_or_yellow_for_ego`, release on K = 2 consecutive `green_for_ego`) + row R5 (fallback: a held stop older than T_max = 25 s, or held on an
answer older than TTL = 2.5 s, is dropped). The light answer comes from Qwen3-VL-4B-Instruct, zero-shot, one forward pass scoring the four
options at the answer position, both cameras (wide + road), 1153 visual tokens; no trained head, no truncation. The batch is the existing 19
routes x 2 traffic seeds (38 runs); `drive` and `pred` (privileged red light) are the runs already in `$DATA_DIR/runs/vlm_arb/arms` and are
not rerun.

## 4. Serving, latency under load, L

CARLA runs synchronously and the agent turns each answer's measured wall-clock latency into simulated delay, so a queue in front of the model
becomes stale answers. Design: one server per card, serving only that card's routes; a quiet request takes 127 ms (capacity ~7.9 requests/s per card);
a route asks every 0.5 s of simulated time. The earlier drive runs advanced at 0.3-0.7x real time (system / game duration 1.5-3.0 over the
19 routes), i.e. 0.7-1.4 requests per second per route, so three routes per card load a server to about 25-55%.

Registered rules (written before the calibration run):
1. Calibration: three shadow units (D24), one per card, 3 workers each, routes of seed 1, the shards of D23, stage-1 provisional L = 0.5 s (only the shadow
   log uses it). In-loop latency of every answer of the three units: p50, p95, p99, share > 0.5 s, share > TTL.
2. **L = ceil(p95 of the calibration latency / 50 ms) x 0.05 s** (the rule of D8, applied to the in-loop distribution).
3. If the calibration p95 is above the registered 600 ms line, the concurrency is reduced to 2 workers per card and the calibration repeated once; if that also
   fails the study stops and reports. Stale answers are not accepted by raising L or TTL.
4. The batch starts only with L written into `gates/vred_L_registered` after this file (with L in section 7) is pushed.
5. Batch monitor (the `few` check after the three seed-0 units, before seed 1): in-batch answered >= 98%, p95 <= 600 ms, share > TTL <= 1%. If it fails, seed 1 does not start; the
   concurrency is reduced and the study resumed, and the failed units stay in the log.
6. Reported with the result: the in-batch latency distribution, the share of answers slower than L (these take effect at t_q + latency) and the share older than the TTL.

## 5. Staged launch and checklist

1. One unit, `dbg-vred-qwen-s0-334`: the real VLM drives R2 + R5 on the debug red-light route 334 (provisional L = 0.5 s). Checklist (existing `red_stop` kind): all routes
   finished, no program crash, finite speeds, >= 98% of requests answered, every answer used no earlier than t_q + L, a stop held by R2 before the line, a roll-off after its release,
   no rule before two answers had arrived.
2. The calibration run (D24, three units, the concurrency of the batch), then the L rule above.
3. Three units of seed 0 (`vred-s0-q0..q2`), then `few`: at least one stop driven by a VLM answer, at least one release after green, no crash, the latency monitor of rule 5, and the
   R5 count recorded. Then the three units of seed 1 and the report.
Every unit also runs the standard unit checklist (`vlm_arb_checks.py`: finished, no crash, finite speeds, request accounting, delay respected).
Chain: `scripts/vlm_vred_chain.py` (one `jevdrive.cl` lane file, root `$DATA_DIR/runs/vlm_arb_vred`, STATUS / DONE / ERROR, resumable by rerunning the command); `--arg stage=pre` then `--arg stage=all`.

## 6. Readouts (registered before the batch)

Conventions of `results/report.md`: official infractions; paired differences per (route, seed) averaged over seeds per route, resampling routes, 2000 draws, seed 0, 95% percentile intervals.
- `vred - drive` on all routes, on routes with a traffic light (the scenario-type set of report.md: 27043, 15102, 28147, 16390, 15612, 15483; primary) and on the others; sensitivity: the same split by routes on which an ego light was seen within 50 m in the `vred` logs (the stop-sign routes 16508 and 16529 have traffic lights too); DS, RC, red-light infractions,
  blocked, collisions, mean speed. Harm on the other routes: DS, runs with more blocked events, runs with more collisions than their `drive` pair.
- Retention (`vred` - `drive`) / (`pred` - `drive`) for DS and for red-light infractions (ratio of route means, route-cluster bootstrap), on all routes and on the light routes.
- In-loop reading quality: answers against the simulator's light state logged during the `vred` runs, the Phase A rows (note: the car stops at red lights, so the frames are not the shadow ones).
- Green-after-red release delay: seconds from the ego light turning green to the second consecutive green answer in force, to the end of R2, and to rolling.
- Per-route table; R5 episodes with cause; every remaining red-light infraction with its cause from the logs (not answered red / answered late / stale answer / R5 fallback / released early / light beyond range / other).
- At 19 routes every number is a diagnostic read. The registered confirmation lines (harmless: CI lower bound of the non-target DS difference >= -5, no new blocked, no new collision; useful: DS CI lower bound
  > 0, red-light infractions fall, retention >= 50%) are printed with their value and marked "not evaluated at this size"; none is reported as passed.

## 7. Wall-clock estimate

Route time of the 19 routes of seed 1 in the earlier drive runs summed to 4387 s of system time (mean 231 s, max 779 s; slowed by the 3 s openjev delays). With 9 workers and a fast VLM
one seed is about 8-10 min including server starts, so the batch (two seeds) ~20-25 min, stage 1 ~3 min, the calibration ~10 min, analysis a few minutes: about 45-60 min of box time
(estimate, not measured). Phase A replay took 6 min on three cards (3281 requests at 127 ms, replayed serially per card).
