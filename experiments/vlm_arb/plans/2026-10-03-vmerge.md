## vmerge-j: follow-up on the light-route loss (registered before its first run, 03:40 JST)

Two mechanisms read from the vmerge logs (seed 0 and seed 1 so far; light routes vmerge - vred -15.0 [-25.0, -5.0] on seed 0):
1. **Green window too short from the stop-line target.** 15483 and 15612 (seed 0) were released on a true green (cusum, at 30.1 / 15.1 s), but
   the light turned red again before the tail cleared the scorer's line (the junction entrance + 5.9 m). The estimated stop line puts the
   car about 3.3 m further back than vred's junction-entrance target; vred (junction target) had 15483 at DS 100 on both seeds and 15612
   seed 0 at 100, while vred2 / vred3 (stop-line target) lost 4 runs to this class (release_replay.md: releasing at the very green onset
   would not have cleared, margins -0.2 to -3.1 s). **Choice: R2's target back to the junction entrance**, here the route-command start
   (rear axle to it minus bumper minus 0.5 m, as vred), `VLM_R2_TARGET=junction`. Chosen over "stop line, then creep to the entrance after N s"
   because it has direct evidence on these very routes and adds no parameter; the known price is the reading under the light (vred: ego-green
   recall while holding 28.6% vs 94.9% at the stop line, and 3 false releases past the line on 27297 / 15612 s1), which the cusum release
   is expected to soften but not remove. R3 keeps the stop-line target (17280 with V2 had no stop infraction).
2. **R5 releases into a long red.** 15102 (both seeds) held at the line, the red lasted 43.5 s from the hold start (identical in all 6
   logged vred / vred2 / vred3 runs), R5 dropped the hold at T_max = 25 s (t = 39 s) and the car ran the red. Logged hold-start-to-green
   waits over 63 hold episodes (release_replay_episodes.csv): max 43.5 s (15102), then 34.7 (24944), 28.4 (15483 / 16529); 15 episodes
   exceed 25 s. **Choice: T_max = 50 s** (`VM_R5_TMAX=50`), the longest logged red plus 6.5 s. Cost: R5 fired in no other vmerge run
   (all 34 runs checked); in vred it rescued one car (15612 s0: the model kept missing the green for 37 s, R5 released at 25 s); with
   T_max 50 such a car would wait up to 25 s longer (time, not an infraction, unless the route timeout is reached; cusum makes the
   missed-green case rarer). R5's "answer older than the TTL" branch is unchanged.
