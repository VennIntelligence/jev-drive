   same-direction traffic, what is the DS on the four obstacle routes (the estimate of what a correct bypass is worth), and does the loss on the 15 other routes go away?
2. `vred` (decision 86): does moving R2's stop target from the junction entrance to the light's stop line stop the car short of the line with the light in view, remove the

New arms: `pbyp2`, `vred2`. Reused, not rerun: `drive`, `pred`, `pbyp`, `vred` (38 runs each under `$DATA_DIR/runs/vlm_arb/arms/`). Same 19 routes (10 dev + 3 red-light + 3

## 2. Fix A: `pbyp2` (code: `lib/b2d_privileged_geometry.py` and its source copy `experiments/b2d_privileged/lib/b2d_privileged_geometry.py`)

Everything of `pbyp` stays (current-state geometry, snapshot at 5 Hz, blocker = static vehicle within -5..50 m ahead with lateral offset below one lane, not on a junction
waypoint, not the head of a red-light queue, bypass path with 2.5-4.5 m shift, ramp 15 m before, return 8 m after, gap_margin / min-gap of the oncoming-lane check).
Changes, selected by the arm name `pbyp2` only (older arms are bit-identical in behaviour):

1. **Projection fix.** `project_ext` extends the first and last route segments to infinity, so an actor behind the first or beyond the last route point gets s < 0 or
   s > route length. A blocker needs 0 <= s <= route length. (`project` clamped to the end points and the lateral test then passed: 22 of the 26 old misfires.)
2. **Static for at least 5 s** (was 2 s): `stationary_s` >= `v2_static_s` = 5 s (speed <= 0.2 m/s, counted from the first snapshot within 100 m).
3. **Red-light memory**: no new bypass state while the ego light was red or yellow within 50 m at any plan step of the last `v2_red_memory_s` = 5 s (an existing state is not
   cancelled). The old junction-proximity condition (blocker not on a junction waypoint) is kept.
4. **Same-direction gap check** (current state only, no scripted future), `same_direction_gap`: for the lane the bypass pulls into when it is a same-direction lane, any
   vehicle (not the blocker group) whose centre is within `sd_lane_tol_m` of the target lane's lateral offset and whose route-parallel speed is >= -0.5 m/s closes the gap when
   its longitudinal extent overlaps [ego rear bumper - `sd_back_m` - closing x `sd_headway_s`, ego front bumper + `sd_ahead_m`] (route arc, rear axle at s_ego, rear bumper
   1.06 m behind it, front bumper 3.84 m ahead of it; closing = max(0, v_vehicle - v_ego)): alongside, just ahead, or behind and arriving within the headway. While the gap is
   closed and the ego has not yet shifted `sd_commit_frac` of the lateral offset, the path is the route itself (the ego stays in or returns to its lane); once it is past that
   share the manoeuvre is committed and the check no longer holds it. The oncoming-lane check of `pbypgap` (borrowed lane, checked before the start only) is on as well.
   Not covered (stated, not fixed here): vehicles entering the lane during the return to the original lane, the oncoming-lane check while shifted.

Parameters (written before any run; the one permitted adjustment is in section 6):

| name | initial (written before the first run) | **frozen** (after the one debug adjustment, section 9) | meaning |
|:--|--:|--:|:--|
| `v2_static_s` | 5 s | 5 s | blocker static time |
| `v2_red_memory_s` | 5 s | 5 s | memory of a red / yellow ego light within 50 m |
| `sd_back_m` | 10 m | **5 m** | clear distance behind the ego rear bumper even for a vehicle that is not closing |
| `sd_ahead_m` | 6 m | **5 m** | clear distance ahead of the ego front bumper |
| `sd_headway_s` | 4 s | **1 s** | time headway of a closing vehicle behind (closing speed x headway added to `sd_back_m`) |
| `sd_lane_tol_m` | 2 m | 2 m | lateral tolerance around the target lane's offset (as the oncoming check) |
| `sd_commit_frac` | 0.5 | **0.3** | share of the lateral shift after which the gap check stops holding the manoeuvre |

Unit tests on synthetic geometry before any CARLA run: `tests/test_bypass2.py` (projection with and without the fix, gap check alongside / ahead / behind / oncoming / excluded /
behind the route start, activation through `Privileged.geometry` with a fake agent: blocker behind the start, beyond the end, static 3 s vs 5 s, red-light memory, gap hold,
open, commit).

## 3. Fix B: `vred2` (code: `lib/vlm_arb_agent.py`, env `VLM_R2_TARGET=stopline`)

R2's stop target moves from the junction entrance to the stop line of the traffic light that governs the ego lane at the next junction on the route: the stop waypoints of every
light actor that lie within 2 m of the route (`get_stop_waypoints`, map information, projected on the route), the line used is the last one from 15 m before the junction entrance
up to 1 m inside it. Target = bumper-to-line distance - 0.5 m, the margin convention of the privileged `pred` (`stop(d - .5)`); the light's state is not read. If the map gives no
line for the junction the entrance is used (logged as `r2_src`). R2's own conditions (`line < 50 m`, `can_stop`, "past the line" drop at -2 m) now refer to this target. Nothing else of
R2 changes: K = 2, release on K = 2 greens, TTL, R5, L = 0.35 s, Qwen3-VL-4B zero-shot, one pass, 1153 tokens, one server per card, FIFO batch 1.

## 5. Latency

The Qwen servers are exactly the `vred` ones (one per card, port 8200 + card, batch 1, FIFO). Registered L = 0.35 s (the `vred` value). Before the batch, a shadow `drive` calibration
(`cal2`, seed 1, all 19 routes, 3 workers per card) is run **at the load of the batch**, i.e. together with the `pbyp2` seed-0 units (6 workers per card): L = 0.35 s holds only if the
in-loop p95 is <= 350 ms, the share above the 2.5 s TTL <= 1% and >= 98% of the requests are answered (`gates/v2_cal.json`, the `calibrate` job passes or fails the chain). If it fails,
the study stops and the concurrency is reduced rather than L or the TTL raised. The `few-vred2` gate re-checks the same lines on the first batch units.

## 6. Staged launch and checklist

Chain: `scripts/vlm_v2_chain.py` (lane `vlm-v2`, root `$DATA_DIR/runs/vlm_arb_v2`, STATUS / DONE / ERROR, resumable). Steps:
1. `--arg stage=pre`: three single-route units, one per card: `dbg-pbyp2-s0-25169`, `dbg-pbyp2-s0-24955` (debug obstacle routes, not in the evaluation set) and `dbg-vred2-s0-334` (debug
   red-light route). Checklist `pbyp2`: obstacle detected, shifted path enabled with a 2.5-4.5 m shift, the car passes and returns to the route, no activation on a blocker outside the
   route; `vred2`: R2 stops with the bumper 0-3.5 m short of the line (stop target in the log is the stop line), rolls after the release, no crash, >= 98% requests answered. Parameters of
   section 2 may be adjusted once on these debug routes, then they are frozen: the adjustment (if any) is committed in section 9 before the batch starts.
2. `--arg stage=all`: `cal2` x3 shadow units together with `pbyp2-s0-q0..q2` (calibration at the load of the batch); `calibrate` (section 5) and `few-pbyp2` (checklist over the
   seed-0 pbyp2 units: no crash, no activation on a blocker outside the route, no activation before the ego first moves on routes without a scenario obstacle, a bypass that returns
   to the lane on at least one obstacle route).
3. `vred2-s0-q0..q2` together with `pbyp2-s1-q0..q2`; then `few-vred2` (a stop short of the line, a release after green, no crash, latency lines). 4. `vred2-s1-q0..q2`, then `report`.
Every unit also runs the standard unit checklist (`vlm_arb_checks.py`). Shards are the `vred` shards (`gates/vred_shards.json`, routes dealt by decreasing `drive` game time).

Reading of the checklist item "no activation before the ego first moves": on a route with a scenario obstacle the obstacle is static from the first frame and 5 s of static time is reached at
t = 5.05 s, before the ego is released (about 6 s); that activation is on a blocker inside the route and is not a defect. The item is checked on routes without a scenario obstacle.

## 9. Execution log

- 16:07 lease `vlm-v2` (3 cards, 6 workers per card), the three Qwen servers up (tmux `vq2-srv`, supervisor, run dir `$DATA_DIR/runs/vlm_arb_v2/qwen`); pre stage started.
- 16:08-16:15 pre stage. `dbg-vred2-s0-334` passed (R2 stopped 3.2 m short of the stop line by the truth distance, the log's stop target is the stop line, released after green; same
  collision with a vehicle as the `vred` debug run). Both `pbyp2` debug units failed the checklist: obstacle detected, but the path was never enabled. The same-direction gap check
  held the car in its lane for the whole run (route timeout, RC 34.8 / 34.7): on both debug routes a stream of vehicles passes in the target lane every 3-4 s at 10-14 m/s;
  the initial rule (10 m + 4 s x closing speed) needs about 54 m clear behind, the spacing of the stream is about 45 m, so the gap never opens (replayed on the logged snapshots:
  0.0% of the time open at the initial values; no window longer than 0.6 s at 10 m / 2 s). That is a deadlock of the parameters, not of the logic. **The one permitted adjustment**:
  `sd_back_m` 5 m, `sd_headway_s` 1 s, `sd_ahead_m` 5 m, `sd_commit_frac` 0.3. Reason, written before the rerun: 5 m + 1 s x closing speed is the distance a follower needs to stop
  with a 4 m/s^2 deceleration at the stream's closing speed (about 11 m/s: 11^2 / 8 = 15 m against 5 + 11 = 16 m) plus a standstill margin, i.e. the usual lane-change safe-braking
  criterion, and the ego reaches 0.3 of the lateral shift within about 1.2 s from standstill (measured on the old pbyp debug run), which is why the commit share is lowered with
  the headway. Frozen from here on; no further change before or during the batch.
- 16:17-16:25 second debug round with the frozen parameters (`dbg2-pbyp2-*`). `dbg2-pbyp2-s0-25169` passed every check: the gap held the car in its lane several times while vehicles passed
  (first activation 7.1 s, path first enabled 15.9 s), the car committed at 0.34 of the shift (28.7 s), passed the obstacle and returned to the route (lateral 0.09 m at 36.9 s). Official:
  1 collision with a same-direction vehicle (nissan patrol, id 192) at about 29.5 s, 0.8 s after the commit while abeam the obstacle (a vehicle that arrived after the check stopped holding),
  DS 60, RC 100 (first round: stuck, DS 34.8). `dbg2-pbyp2-s0-24955` did **not** pass:
  the car reached the cones at 46 m with the gap closing again and again (open windows of 0.2-0.8 s between vehicles arriving every 3.4 s at 13.4 m/s), crept to 0.2 of the shift at standstill
  and stayed 190 s in front of the cones (collision with a cone, route timeout, DS 22.5, RC 34.7). This is the frozen gap rule meeting sustained traffic when the car has already stopped in front of the
  obstacle (from standstill the lateral shift is slow), not an implementation fault; it is not tuned further (one adjustment only). It is waived as a dependency of the batch (`WAIVED` in the chain) and
  kept as evidence; whether it recurs on the evaluation routes is a result of the batch.
- 16:26-16:42 stage `all`, step 2: `pbyp2-s0-q0..q2` and `cal2-s1-q0..q2` together (6 workers per card, units took 11-16 min). `few-pbyp2` passed (no crash, no activation on a blocker outside the route,
