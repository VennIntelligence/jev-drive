## 2. Fix A: `pbyp2` (code: `lib/b2d_privileged_geometry.py` and its source copy `experiments/b2d_privileged/lib/b2d_privileged_geometry.py`)

Everything of `pbyp` stays (current-state geometry, snapshot at 5 Hz, blocker = static vehicle within -5..50 m ahead with lateral offset below one lane, not on a junction
waypoint, not the head of a red-light queue, bypass path with 2.5-4.5 m shift, ramp 15 m before, return 8 m after, gap_margin / min-gap of the oncoming-lane check).
Changes, selected by the arm name `pbyp2` only (older arms are bit-identical in behaviour):

1. **Projection fix.** `project_ext` extends the first and last route segments to infinity, so an actor behind the first or beyond the last route point gets s < 0 or
   s > route length. A blocker needs 0 <= s <= route length. (`project` clamped to the end points and the lateral test then passed: 22 of the 26 old misfires.)
2. **Static for at least 5 s** (was 2 s): `stationary_s` >= `v2_static_s` = 5 s (speed <= 0.2 m/s, counted from the first snapshot within 100 m).
3. **Red-light memory**: no new bypass state while the ego light was red or yellow within 50 m at any plan step of the last `v2_red_memory_s` = 5 s (an existing state is not
   cancelled). The old junction-proximity condition (blocker not on a junction waypoint) is kept.
4. **Same-direction gap check** (current state only, no scripted future), `same_direction_gap`: for the lane the bypass pulls into when it is a same-direction lane, any
   vehicle (not the blocker group) whose centre is within `sd_lane_tol_m` of the target lane's lateral offset and whose route-parallel speed is >= -0.5 m/s closes the gap when
   its longitudinal extent overlaps [ego rear bumper - `sd_back_m` - closing x `sd_headway_s`, ego front bumper + `sd_ahead_m`] (route arc, rear axle at s_ego, rear bumper
   1.06 m behind it, front bumper 3.84 m ahead of it; closing = max(0, v_vehicle - v_ego)): alongside, just ahead, or behind and arriving within the headway. While the gap is
   closed and the ego has not yet shifted `sd_commit_frac` of the lateral offset, the path is the route itself (the ego stays in or returns to its lane); once it is past that
   share the manoeuvre is committed and the check no longer holds it. The oncoming-lane check of `pbypgap` (borrowed lane, checked before the start only) is on as well.
   Not covered (stated, not fixed here): vehicles entering the lane during the return to the original lane, the oncoming-lane check while shifted.

Parameters (written before any run; the one permitted adjustment is in section 6):

| name | initial (written before the first run) | **frozen** (after the one debug adjustment, section 9) | meaning |
|:--|--:|--:|:--|
| `v2_static_s` | 5 s | 5 s | blocker static time |
| `v2_red_memory_s` | 5 s | 5 s | memory of a red / yellow ego light within 50 m |
| `sd_back_m` | 10 m | **5 m** | clear distance behind the ego rear bumper even for a vehicle that is not closing |
| `sd_ahead_m` | 6 m | **5 m** | clear distance ahead of the ego front bumper |
| `sd_headway_s` | 4 s | **1 s** | time headway of a closing vehicle behind (closing speed x headway added to `sd_back_m`) |
| `sd_lane_tol_m` | 2 m | 2 m | lateral tolerance around the target lane's offset (as the oncoming check) |
| `sd_commit_frac` | 0.5 | **0.3** | share of the lateral shift after which the gap check stops holding the manoeuvre |

behind the route start, activation through `Privileged.geometry` with a fake agent: blocker behind the start, beyond the end, static 3 s vs 5 s, red-light memory, gap hold,

## 3. Fix B: `vred2` (code: `lib/vlm_arb_agent.py`, env `VLM_R2_TARGET=stopline`)

R2's stop target moves from the junction entrance to the stop line of the traffic light that governs the ego lane at the next junction on the route: the stop waypoints of every
light actor that lie within 2 m of the route (`get_stop_waypoints`, map information, projected on the route), the line used is the last one from 15 m before the junction entrance
up to 1 m inside it. Target = bumper-to-line distance - 0.5 m, the margin convention of the privileged `pred` (`stop(d - .5)`); the light's state is not read. If the map gives no
line for the junction the entrance is used (logged as `r2_src`). R2's own conditions (`line < 50 m`, `can_stop`, "past the line" drop at -2 m) now refer to this target. Nothing else of
R2 changes: K = 2, release on K = 2 greens, TTL, R5, L = 0.35 s, Qwen3-VL-4B zero-shot, one pass, 1153 tokens, one server per card, FIFO batch 1.

## 5. Latency

The Qwen servers are exactly the `vred` ones (one per card, port 8200 + card, batch 1, FIFO). Registered L = 0.35 s (the `vred` value). Before the batch, a shadow `drive` calibration
(`cal2`, seed 1, all 19 routes, 3 workers per card) is run **at the load of the batch**, i.e. together with the `pbyp2` seed-0 units (6 workers per card): L = 0.35 s holds only if the
in-loop p95 is <= 350 ms, the share above the 2.5 s TTL <= 1% and >= 98% of the requests are answered (`gates/v2_cal.json`, the `calibrate` job passes or fails the chain). If it fails,
the study stops and the concurrency is reduced rather than L or the TTL raised. The `few-vred2` gate re-checks the same lines on the first batch units.

## 11. Added arm `vred3`: `vred2` + junction slow-down on the approach + a yellow / commit rule (registered before it runs)

Brief addition (coordinator, after the first `vred2` / `pbyp2` work): 4 of the 6 red-light infractions left in `vred` are not reading errors (16390: the light turned red just after the car had started
crossing; 27297, two seeds: yellow 5 m before the line at 5 m/s, the car braked and stopped 1.8 m past the line). What the scorer does, read in
`third_party/Bench2Drive/scenario_runner/srunner/scenariomanager/scenarioatomics/atomic_criteria.py`, `RunningRedLightTest`: only the Red state counts (yellow is free); the light's line is the lane's last
waypoint before the intersection (`get_traffic_light_waypoints` advances the trigger-box waypoints until `is_intersection`), i.e. the junction entrance, not the light's stop waypoint; the test fires
while the light is Red and the segment from 0.8 x half length to (half length + 1 m) behind the vehicle centre (1.96 to 3.45 m behind it) crosses that line. A car whose front crossed on green or yellow
is therefore still penalised if its tail has not passed the line when the light turns red: it has cleared the line when the front bumper is 2.45 + 3.45 = 5.9 m beyond the junction entrance, and a car that
stops with its front less than 4.4 m beyond the entrance line is not penalised.

Arm `vred3` = `vred2` (stop-line target) + R1 + R2 first-answer handling + the yellow decision + the commit rule + R5. Same 19 routes x 2 traffic seeds, same serving (per-card Qwen3-VL-4B servers, L = 0.35 s,
3 workers per card, nothing else on the box). Code: `lib/yellow_rule.py` (pure functions), `lib/vlm_arb_agent.py` (`VLM_ARM=vred3`, rows `R1,R2,R5`, `VLM_R2_TARGET=stopline`), unit tests
`tests/test_yellow_rule.py` (go / stop decision on a grid of distance x speed x yellow age).

**Rule, as registered**
1. *R1 on the approach only.* Cap 4.5 m/s from 25 m before the junction entrance (as `jslow`), but only until the front bumper passes the light's stop line (the line known from the map; a junction without
   a light stop line keeps the `jslow` extent through the junction). Once the front bumper is past the stop line, and while a "go" is committed, the cap is lifted entirely (the speed target is the normal
   cruise, 8 m/s) until the tail has cleared the line.
2. *Knowing the yellow.* Inferred from the answers (option b of the brief), no model change: a `red_or_yellow_for_ego` answer right after a `green_for_ego` answer starts an encounter; the yellow is at most
   as old as the time since the frame of the last green answer (conservative: it may have started right after it); a last green answer older than `max_age_s` says nothing, the light is then treated as
   red. Duration: **measured**, not assumed (`results/vred3_yellow_offline.md` section 1): 123 of 143 complete green-yellow-red sequences of the logged lights (13 routes, 64 lights) have exactly 6 yellow
   samples of 0.5 s, i.e. 3.0 s; the 16 with 7 samples bracket 3.5 s at most; `yellow_s` = 3.0 s. Option (a), a yellow option in the question, was not run: it needs a changed prompt and server and a new Phase A,
   and there are only 48 yellow frames (7 onsets, three repeats of the same traffic seed) to validate it on; option (b) works on the unchanged serving: of the 48 yellow frames 87.5% are answered
   `red_or_yellow_for_ego`, and 6 of 7 yellow onsets were answered non-green on the first yellow frame (the 7th two frames late; there the last green answer was 16.5 s old, which the `max_age_s` rule
   turns into "treat as red"). The earliest-onset age deficit (true age minus assumed age) was 0.0 s in all 6 cases where the rule applies.
3. *Go or stop at the first non-green answer* (`yellow_rule.decide`): with `d_stop` = front bumper to the stop target (stop line - 0.5 m), `d_line` = front bumper to the scorer's line (junction entrance),
   v = speed, remaining = `yellow_s` - `lag_margin_s` - age: **stop** if v x `tau_s` + v^2 / (2 `a_comfort`) <= `d_stop` (comfortable stop incl. reaction delay, preferred); else **go** if the whole car
   clears the scorer's line before the red, i.e. the front bumper covers `d_line` + 5.9 m + `tail_margin_m` within the remaining yellow (planner latency `t_go_s` at the current speed, then `a_go` up to the
   cruise speed); else **stop** (hard: the R2 stop constraint at the strongest the table has; if even that cannot stop before the target, the car continues as a go). "Go" = no braking and R2 is suppressed
   until the tail has cleared the line (front bumper 5.9 m beyond the line, 10 s at most).
4. *Commit.* Once the front bumper has passed the light's stop line an R2 stop is never started (the old rule allowed a start up to 0.5 m beyond it at walking speed); R1 is off (item 1).
5. *Debounce.* Only for a green -> non-green transition (the case where the delay costs the stop line) the table does not wait for K = 2: at the first non-green answer the decision of item 3 is taken; a
   "stop" starts R2 tentatively (the same IDM stop constraint, i.e. a gentle deceleration towards the stop target), the second answer confirms it (non-green) or cancels it (anything else: hold dropped,
   release as usual). Every other start stays on K = 2. Offline cost (`vred3_yellow_offline.md` section 3): in 408 green frames of 26 approaches there is 1 green -> non-green answer transition before the
   stop line, and it persisted (0 single flickers that a tentative start would have started and cancelled); 10 more happen after the front bumper had passed the line, where the commit rule starts nothing.
   Expected cost in the batch: tentative starts / cancellations are counted in the logs (`k = "y"` lines) and reported.

Parameters (initial = frozen unless section 9 says otherwise; one adjustment permitted on debug routes outside the 19):

| name | value | basis |
|:--|--:|:--|
| `yellow_s` | 3.0 s | measured, offline section 1 |
| `lag_margin_s` | 0.5 s | subtracted from the remaining yellow: covers a model that answers green for one more frame (0.5 s); offline deficit 0.0 s in 6 of 6 |
| `max_age_s` | 2.0 s | a last green answer older than this = treat as red |
| `a_comfort` | 3.0 m/s^2 | comfortable deceleration |
| `tau_s` | 0.5 s | plan-to-actuation delay of the stop option (the answer delay is inside the age) |
| `TAIL_M` | 5.90 m | front bumper beyond the junction entrance at which the scorer's tail segment has passed (read from the scorer) |
| `tail_margin_m` | 0.5 m | extra clearance of the go option |
| `a_go`, `t_go_s` | 1.0 m/s^2, 0.3 s | mild acceleration up to the cruise speed (8 m/s), planner latency |
| R1 | 25 m, 4.5 m/s | as `jslow` |
| first-answer rule | transition only | item 5 |

Hypothetical decisions on real states (logged `vred` encounters, `vred3_yellow_offline.md` section 4): 27297 (both seeds, v = 5.3 / 4.7 m/s, 4.8 m to the stop target, 7.3-7.6 m to the scorer's line): `stop_hard`
(a comfortable stop needs 7.4 / 6.0 m, the clear needs about 14 m in 1.65 s remaining); 24944 (15-18 m to the target): `stop`. So the 27297 encounters are decided "stop, hard"; what changes against `vred` is
the 0.5 s saved by the first-answer start and the slower approach (R1), not the decision.

## 12. Inputs of the arbitration rows by source (CARLA map API through `CarlaDataProvider` against the official route) (list asked for; no code change)

Official route (leaderboard `set_global_plan`, the dense plan with its road commands; `Route` in `scripts/b2d_zeroshot_agent.py`): route polyline xy, arc length s, the commands (turns, lane changes), and with it
the ego's progress `ego_s` (projection of the agent's filtered pose on the route). Ego pose and speed come from the agent's own sensors (GNSS, IMU, speedometer, rear-axle offset), camera frames from the

CARLA map / world API through `CarlaDataProvider` (`Privileged.prepare`, `VlmArbAgent._prepare` / `_stop_arcs`):

- **stop-line position** of the light that governs a route lane: `get_stop_waypoints()` of every `*traffic_light*` actor in the world, those within 2 m of the route, projected on it (map geometry; the light's state is never
  read for it): R2 target (`vred2`, `vred3`), the end of R1's approach and the commit test (`vred3`);

- adjacent lanes (`wp.get_left_lane / get_right_lane`, lane type, offset, direction) and, for the bypass, the states of every vehicle and static prop from the world snapshot (a privileged actor list, not available to a
  real agent): `Privileged.geometry` / `adjacent` for R4 and for the `pbyp*` arms;
- evaluation only, never a control input of the vred arms: the light state and distance (`ctx.tl`, `tl_dist`), lead gap, near actors (`_ctx`), the labels in `vlm_decisions.jsonl` (the oracle answers of the debug units
  are the only way truth reaches the table, `VLM_ORACLE`, debug routes only).
