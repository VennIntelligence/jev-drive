# Stop-position probe on frozen openpilot (Cinque): pre-registration

Written 2026-10-03 before any feature was extracted and before any probe number was computed. Offline only: no CARLA run, no closed loop, no change to driving code. Results go to `../results/stoppos.md`; code is `../scripts/stoppos_*.py`.

**Question.** Can the frozen fast driving model tell how far the junction entrance and the traffic light's stop line are, so that the car can creep up to the line without the simulator's map? The official route already gives the junction entrance to about 1 m (route_junction_error.md) but has no stop line (the line lies a median 4.2 m, up to 10.2 m, before the entrance), and the light is read reliably only when the car stands before the line.

What I looked at before writing this (counts and label quality only, no model output): the number of approaches in the logs (below), and the agreement of the map-derived labels with the logged CARLA truth on the 13 closed-loop junctions (section Labels).

P5 worlds (obstacle scenarios on the same base routes) are not used: obstacles confound the scene and add no new junctions. No route of the 19 closed-loop evaluation routes is in P4 (checked). The existing P5 / P6 frame features (`night_queue_2`) hold only the indexed target frames, not whole approaches, so they are not reused; every stream is re-run from a zero state like an engaged car.

## Labels (training / evaluation targets only, never model input)

All distances in metres along the route, from the **front bumper**, positive ahead. A frame is an *approach frame* of a junction when its bumper is at most 40 m before the junction entrance; frames more than 3.8 m past the entrance / more than 2 m past the stop line are *past* and excluded from the distance tasks.

- CL, from the agent logs (`vlm_decisions.jsonl`, the answer record at the frame's query time): `d_junc` = `gt.junc_dist` - 3.8394 (rear axle to bumper; the map flag of the first junction route point), `d_stop` = `gt.tl_dist` (CARLA stop waypoint of the governing light, bumper to line), `d_sign` = `gt.stop_dist`, light state `gt.tl`, ego speed and route progress from the step record. `has_light` / `has_sign` of a junction come from whether a stop waypoint / stop-sign trigger of the route lies within the agent's window of the junction entrance.
- P4, from the route and pose logs plus the OpenDRIVE file of the town (`stoppos_xodr.py`; no simulator): ego arc = projection of the logged pose on the route polyline, bumper = centre + 2.4508 m; `d_junc` = entrance arc (first route point whose nearest driving-lane sample lies on a junction road) - bumper arc; `has_light` / `has_sign` = a traffic-light post / stop sign within 35 m of the entrance point; `d_stop` = painted stop line on the route in [entrance - 15, entrance + 1] m minus 3.0 m minus bumper arc, only where the town has painted lines (Town12, 13, 15) and the junction has a light; elsewhere `d_stop` is unknown and the frame is left out of the stop-line tasks.
- **Label validation (made before this plan, on the 13 closed-loop junctions, logged truth vs map-derived):** entrance: 8 of 13 within 0.8 m, 12 of 13 within 2.5 m, one start-clamped junction at 3.0 m (the seven Town12 junctions mean |error| 1.5 m, the other six 0.3 m); light presence 13 of 13 correct; the painted line is 3.0 m after CARLA's stop waypoint with sd 0.3 m on the 8 Town12 / Town15 junctions that have one (n = 8), so `d_stop` of P4 carries about 0.3 m of label noise plus the entrance noise; Town13's offset is assumed equal (unverified). Report: P4 labels are map-derived and approximate to about 1 m; CL labels are the agent's truth.
- Route-command baseline input: `d_cmd` = arc of the first LEFT / RIGHT / STRAIGHT command point of the dense official plan (as logged in `route.json`; P4: `option_name`) minus the bumper arc; infinity if the route has none.

Tasks and classes (computed from the labels above):

| task | definition |
|:--|:--|
| regress `d_junc` | approach frames of junctions, target clipped to [0, 40] (frames with d in [-3.8, 0) count as 0) |
| regress `d_stop` | approach frames of junctions with a governing light and a known stop line, `d_stop` in [-2, 40] |
| classify `stop40` | positive: a governing light's stop line within [-2, 40] m ahead; negative: everything else that is not *past* (far, no junction, no light) |
| classify `light`, `sign` | on approach frames with `d_junc` in [0, 40]: this junction has a traffic light (stop sign) vs not |
| 4 classes (action) | from `d_stop`: `at_line` d <= 1.5 m; `creep` 1.5 < d <= 6; `slow` 6 < d <= 20; `free` d > 20 m or no governing light line within 40 m (includes junctions without a light) |

## Features (frozen Cinque, `cinque.onnx`, TensorRT fp16, own engine cache for this card, GPU 1 only)

Taps per frame: `temporal` (`select_4`, 512), `vision` (`mean`, 512), `hidden` (the 16 384-d hidden state; reduced to 512 PCA components fitted on the training frames of each fold), `native` (every native output head except the hidden state, 2 066 values: lane lines and probabilities, road edges, meta incl. hard-brake / brake-press, desire prediction, pose, plan, lead, action). No desire pulse and no traffic input (desire = none, as in the P5 recipe), so the route command does not reach the features.

- P4: the P5 recipe unchanged (each stream from a zero state; 5 Hz frames, each held for 4 steps of the 20 Hz clock, read at the last held step).
- CL: only 2 Hz frames were saved (the 20 Hz frames the closed loop saw are not on disk). Each stream is a whole attempt from a zero state; each frame is held for 10 steps and read at the last held step. This is a **deviation**: the model's temporal context is coarser than in the closed loop. Its size is measured, not assumed: the replayed plan speed (at 1, 2, 3 and 5 s), lead probability and lane probabilities are compared with the 20 Hz values logged in `plans.jsonl` at the same query time (correlation and mean absolute difference over all CL frames), reported in the results.
- Estimated wall clock: Cinque 2.4 ms per 20 Hz step measured on GPU 1 (CL 347 k steps about 15 min, P4 77 k steps about 3 min), JPEG decoding and remapping 12 CPU workers about 10 min; the engine build for this card about 5 min. Staged launch: 3 streams of each set, then 1 / 10 / all of each.

## Split

Unit = route (CL route id, P4 route id; ids do not collide). 5-fold route-grouped cross-validation over all routes, stratified by (source, junction kind light / stop sign / none) with seed 0; registered as `b2d/stoppos-cv5-fold{0..4}` (the test routes of each fold; train = the rest), plus `b2d/stoppos-all`. Within the training routes of a fold, 25% of the routes (seeded, same stratification) are the inner calibration set for hyper-parameters, temperature scaling and interval calibration; the final model of the fold is refitted on all its training routes with those hyper-parameters, and the calibration constants stay as fitted. The headline is the pooled out-of-fold prediction (every route predicted by a model that never saw it). Secondary: train on P4 only, test on CL; train on CL only, test on P4 (domain transfer; the two sets differ in cameras and speeds).

## Heads and readouts

1. **Native outputs (question 1).** (a) What the model defines: output slices from the ONNX metadata and `lib/op_arb_agent.py` META layout (no stop-line, traffic-light or junction head). (b) Measured on the `plans.jsonl` logs of all `drive`-mode units (eval-drive-s0, -rep1..3, v2-drive-*; pure openpilot longitudinal with set speed 8 m/s, no VLM): for approach frames with a governing light, the plan speed at 3 s over the ego speed, the plan speed at 5 s, the brake-press probability (`brk`) and the hard-brake-3 probability as functions of `d_stop` in 5 m bins, split by light state (red / yellow / green) and by "no light" junction approaches (`d_junc` bins); medians with route-clustered intervals. A native readout counts as usable if its median moves monotonically with distance by more than the spread between routes in the bins 0-20 m; otherwise not.
2. **Probes (question 2).** Standardised ridge regression and L2 logistic regression on each tap (alpha / C chosen on the inner calibration set from a fixed grid: ridge 1, 10, 100, 1e3, 1e4; C 0.01, 0.1, 1); one small MLP per tap (1 hidden layer of 256 ReLU, dropout 0.1, AdamW weight decay 1e-3, 30 epochs, early stop on the inner set). Metrics per true-distance bin 0-5, 5-10, 10-20, 20-40 m: mean absolute error, signed bias (prediction minus truth), median and 90th percentile absolute error; per route first (mean over the route's attempts of the attempt's mean in the bin), then over routes, with a route-clustered bootstrap interval (`jevdrive.stats.bootstrap`, B = 10 000). Classifiers: AUC, accuracy at the 0.5 threshold, balanced accuracy.
3. **Baselines.** (i) Route command point alone: `d_junc` = `d_cmd`; `d_stop` = `d_cmd` - delta with delta the median (command point - stop line) of the training routes of the fold; `stop40` score = -`d_cmd`; for `light` / `sign` the training prior (no information). (ii) Ego speed / time alone: ridge and gradient boosting (`HistGradientBoostingRegressor`) on [speed, time since the run start, distance driven since the start, and their squares] (the distance driven is an odometer: in P4 most runs start 27 to 29 m before the entrance, so it is a strong clock; this baseline is reported on both sets and on CL alone). (iii) Probe plus clock: ridge on the tap and the clock features, to test what the tap adds. The probe is credited with reading the scene only where it beats (ii) with a route-clustered paired interval that excludes zero.
4. **Discrete head with probabilities and an interval (question 3).** (a) Multinomial logistic on the four action classes with temperature scaling fitted on the inner set; reliability: expected calibration error (10 equal-mass bins, one-vs-rest per class and macro), Brier score, reliability tables, accuracy, confusion matrix. (b) Linear quantile regression (pinball loss) at q = 0.1, 0.5, 0.9 for `d_stop` on the approach frames, with the conformalised quantile correction (CQR) fitted on the inner set for a nominal 80% interval; report empirical coverage overall and per distance bin (also without the correction), mean width. Primary tap for question 3: the tap with the lowest pooled out-of-fold macro MAE of `d_stop` in the bin 0-10 m in step 2 (chosen on the same out-of-fold predictions, so mildly optimistic; all four taps are reported).
5. **Stopping rules replayed (question 3).** Rule U(m): the first frame of the approach at which the upper bound `hi` of the remaining `d_stop` is at most m (m = 2, 4, 6 m); rule L(m): the same with the lower bound; plus the route rule R(m): `d_cmd` - delta <= m. Replayed on held-out light approaches along the logged trajectories (open loop: the car did not actually obey the rule). Reported per approach: true `d_stop` and `d_junc` at the firing frame, the speed v there, and the implied stop point x = `d_stop` - v^2 / (2 a) with a = 3.0 m/s^2 (the base governor's braking limit; x < 0 means past the line), relative to the line and to the entrance; the fraction of approaches in which the rule never fires before the line is passed; distributions as quantiles (min, 10, 50, 90, max) over approaches, split CL / P4.
6. **Data size (question 4).** Train on n routes drawn at random from the training routes of each fold, n = 10, 20, 40, 80 and all (20 draws for the small n, 5 for 80), test on the fold's routes; report the macro MAE of `d_stop` for 0-10 m and 10-40 m, and the `stop40` AUC against n and against the number of light approaches in the training draw (tap = the primary tap; ridge and logistic).

## What would count as what (question 5), fixed before the numbers

- **Replaces the map for the stop position:** pooled out-of-fold median |error| of `d_stop` <= 1.0 m and p90 <= 2.5 m in the bin 0-10 m on both CL and P4, 80% interval coverage within 72-88% in every bin, `stop40` balanced accuracy >= 0.9 and `light` accuracy >= 0.9, and better than the route baseline (command point - delta) with a paired interval that excludes zero.
- **Refinement on top of the route's junction point:** the probe beats the route baseline for `d_stop` in 0-10 m with a paired interval excluding zero and beats the clock baseline, but misses the numbers above.
- **Not usable:** otherwise.
A probe fitted on CARLA frames is in-domain supervised; the report says so, and says which statements rest on the P4 map-derived labels (approximate to about 1 m) and which on the CL agent truth.

## Status labels

*Verified* = read from a log field or a measured run; *inferred* = computed under a stated assumption. The number of independent junctions is small (about 52 light approaches with a stop line in total): every interval is a route-clustered one, and the closed-loop frames of one route are not independent.
