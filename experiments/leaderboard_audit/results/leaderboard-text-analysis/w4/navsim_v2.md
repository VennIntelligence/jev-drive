## 指标公式

- 单帧 `EPDMS = NC×DAC×DDC×TLC × (5EP+5TTC+2LK+2HC+2EC)/16`，若 human 在同一原始场景的某个子项得 0，配置开启时将对应 agent 子项置为 1，再重算乘积和加权数组。NC、DAC、DDC、TLC 是乘积门；EP/TTC/LK/HC/EC 权重见代码。EC 用相邻帧重叠模拟轨迹的动力学一致性；单场景得分在后续注入 EC。[`pdm_scorer.py:40–73,169–215`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/planning/simulation/planner/pdm_planner/scoring/pdm_scorer.py#L40-L73)、[计算 `run_pdm_score.py:187–215`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/planning/script/run_pdm_score.py#L187-L215)、[human 过滤 `pdm_score.py:171–219`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/evaluate/pdm_score.py#L171-L219)、[EC `scene_aggregator.py:49–77`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/planning/simulation/planner/pdm_planner/scoring/scene_aggregator.py#L49-L77)。
- EP 是中心线投影的正向进度，以同一 proposal 组中乘积门通过者的最大进度归一化，门通过者最大值不超过 5 m 时 EP 默认 1；TTC 先查询相交对象，再对停止速度阈值以下的候选跳过失分处理。LK 对中心线横向偏移 >0.5 m 且持续 2 秒记 0，在交叉口跳过此检查。[`pdm_scorer.py:223–251`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/planning/simulation/planner/pdm_planner/scoring/pdm_scorer.py#L223-L251)、[`pdm_scorer.py:476–490,542–554`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/planning/simulation/planner/pdm_planner/scoring/pdm_scorer.py#L476-L490)、[LK `:615–655`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/planning/simulation/planner/pdm_planner/scoring/pdm_scorer.py#L615-L655)。
- v2.2 伪闭环：对原始帧和上一帧分别计算 4 秒得分；其后预生成多条合成后续场景，后续场景起点与首段终点的二维距离以 `exp(−d²/(2×0.1))` 加权。每组得分为“首段得分 × 加权后续得分”，两个时间错位组再平均，最后对场景组平均。[`scene_aggregator.py:18–47,79–124`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/planning/simulation/planner/pdm_planner/scoring/scene_aggregator.py#L18-L47)、[`run_pdm_score.py:242–289`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/planning/script/run_pdm_score.py#L242-L289)。v2.0 实现曾采用另一套后续场景权重与全局加权平均，故需按 devkit 版本报告。[v2.0 权重 `run_pdm_score.py:245–290`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/c0b6fb05d9060f665dc98830285fe236312dc2ba/navsim/planning/script/run_pdm_score.py#L245-L290)、[v2.0 汇总 `:553–558`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/c0b6fb05d9060f665dc98830285fe236312dc2ba/navsim/planning/script/run_pdm_score.py#L553-L558)。

## 盲区与证据界限

后续场景是预先生成并由首段终点加权，**没有按 agent 每一步动作连续重新仿真整个交通世界**；单帧仍以固定 4 秒 horizon 计算。伪闭环聚合比 v1 增加恢复能力代理项，但真实多车互相适应与长时安全仍在直接测量之外。[`docs/metrics.md:53–72`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/docs/metrics.md#L53-L72)、[`scene_aggregator.py:79–123`](https://github.com/autonomousvision/navsim/blob/359c7f72304bfa8273e754224a213d3751bd2340/navsim/planning/simulation/planner/pdm_planner/scoring/scene_aggregator.py#L79-L123)。
