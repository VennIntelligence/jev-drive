## Material

- States: `on1_PAID` (DAgger iteration 1, student R5LC-L18-s0, 718 train scenes, 110 981 rows; row files with anchors, human log, events,
  boxes in `runs/round6/dag/rows/PAID`), `on1_PAID2A` / `on1_PAID2B` (iteration 2, students R6DAG-L18-s0 / s1: their own states). The PAID2

  GPU trainer-path forwards of R6DAG-s0 / s1 on their own states (`dag2/s*/fwd/PAID*-train-rec/plans_*.npz`, every row).
- Rasters: road_pai.py's evaluator-rule surface (`pai_raster`, d418 amendment 1) at every PAID anchor; own output dir.
- Policy: R6DAG-L18-s1 and s0, CPU forward (rtc.py `band` path = the trainer's build with R6DAG's argv) on a seeded uniform sample of PAID rows.
- Terms at trainer weights (ho_w 3): agent 30 x AgentHinge.per_step (margin 0.3, pool boxes), road 30 x relu(0.3 - margin) on road_pai,
  side 3 x nr1_lat side (buf 0.4), onc: none (PAI has no lane-direction raster; reported as unavailable). Values on the eval-mode plan.
- Candidate rows: teacher-labelled DAgger rows (`dagger.npz draw`, v0 >= 2 m/s). Unlearnable = t0 footprint off the surface, or the row

  LA = active and not unlearnable.

## Gate lines (each pass / fail; verdict = all pass)

- **G1 raster validity** (PAID, all rows): (a) the human's own log pose at t0, placed in the student's raster, has its footprint inside
  (min corner SDF >= 0) on >= 97 % of rows with a log pose; (b) evaluator agreement: of the decisions at the evaluator's offroad event
  (first decision at or after `t_event_us` of an offroad zero), >= 70 % read off-surface (t0 footprint SDF < 0) or hinge-active on the
  student's served plan; and rows of scenes with no offroad event read t0 off-surface on <= 2 %; (c) t0 already off the surface (unlearnable)
  on <= 10 % of candidate rows.
- **G2 agent boxes**: box coverage (row has agent label and >= 1 valid box at t0 within the canvas) reported; alignment: the student's t0
  footprint overlaps a box (t0 agent hinge > 0 with margin 0) on <= 2 % of rows in scenes without a collision event; the human's log future
  triggers the agent hinge on <= 20 % of agent-active rows; the teacher label also violates on <= 30 % of agent-active rows.
- **G3 activity** (R6DAG s1 primary, s0 replicate; PAID2 agent / side on own states): LA share of candidate rows >= 10 % (nuPlan S pool
  12.3 %), and hinge mass on learnable rows >= 50 % (nuPlan R6DAG draw 33 %), both checkpoints.
- **G4 coverage of the loss classes**: LA rows above 13 m/s >= 30 % of LA (s1 primary); turns (|teacher 4 s heading change| > 30 deg) at
  4-10 m/s >= 5 % of LA (S pool LA turn rows 1.9-4.8 % of draws); estimated LA rows per batch at a dose of 8 / 16 PAI-S rows reported,
  line >= 2 per batch at 16.
- **G5 label sanity** on a sample of LA road / agent rows (shape-only gradient of the term on the model plan, step 0.2 m max): median
  relative 4 s arc change within +-2 % and median end-speed change within +-0.3 m/s; the step reduces the term on >= 90 % of rows; the
  step moves the plan toward the teacher label (cos > 0) on >= 60 % of road-LA rows; and the share of road-active rows whose teacher label
  also violates is reported (the d420 pitfall).

Expected PAI-281 points: from smix_value Q3 (S-addressable block ~19 + drift ~4.5 pts per seed) scaled by the measured LA coverage per
class relative to S-MIX's, with the S schedule's measured ~5 % hinge movement as the learning ceiling. If all lines pass, the row spec
(pool, weights, terms, dose) to fold into the next DAgger joint arm is written in the result; otherwise no.
