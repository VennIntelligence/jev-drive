# SWV1: does the plan know where the ego body will be? Swept-footprint clearance on the collision rollouts (pre-registration, 2026-10-10)

Lane SWV1. Written and pushed before any number of this lane is computed. Offline only: existing AlpaSim rollouts (nuPlan public scenes on
the GPU box, PAI on the Tokyo box), replays of logged driver inputs through the driver class, kinematic counterfactuals. No closed-loop
simulation, no training, nothing registered or submitted. Simulator boxes, maps, logged paths and recorded object motion are labels and
oracle inputs only; every counterfactual that uses them is a ceiling and is labelled so. No WA-JEPA or borrowed weights.

## Question

The user's reading of COL1's 31 clips: besides driving straight into the vehicle ahead (lanes FIX1 / TR1 / DIAG1), the car does not slow
when it goes around something or turns sharply, and its path does not clear the object: "no estimate of where its own body will be during
a manoeuvre and whether that sweep touches anyone". This lane (A) classifies every at-fault collision by what the plan's own swept
footprint did, (B) reads kinematic counterfactuals, (C) asks which non-privileged output could carry "my sweep will touch something".

How this differs from settled reads (no mechanism of these is re-tested):
- 157 (fixed 2 m lead margin on stored plans), 219 / FIX1 (1-D lead gap -> deceleration cap along the ego heading): longitudinal, gap along
  the heading only. Here the test is the plan's 2-D swept ego box against object boxes, and the lead head's lateral position is used.
- 158 (agent hinge in training), 197 / 200 (occupancy through the memory channel): training arms. Nothing is trained here.
- 179 (self road-edge margin against DAC failure, lead gap against NC / TTC failure) and 203 (slow-down selector; AUC of pooled features
  against "fails and slowing rescues"): navtest open loop, one decision per token, 4 s non-reactive simulator labels. Here: closed-loop
  decisions of our own rollouts, the label is a geometric fact of the served plan itself (its swept ego box intersects a recorded object
  box), the candidate signals are the base model's geometric heads placed against that same sweep, and lead time before a real impact
  is read. Pooled-feature probes (hidden state, vision tokens) are not repeated.
- 205 (closed-loop heading drift), 207 (turn path-shape precision), 218 (speed profile is a function of ego state): used as class
  definitions and context, not re-tested.

## Units

- **C** (collision rollouts): every rollout with `collision_at_fault`. nuPlan: P2H10-F-s0 / s1 (primary, 22) and APY10m10-AB-s0 / s1
  (24, reported separately) from COL1's `cases.pkl`. PAI: P2H10-F-s0 baseline (12) and COL1's re-timed arm `v1` (its collisions, separately).
- **N** (clean rollouts): nuPlan: COL1's control set (P2H10-F-s0, part001 + part002, no collision flag; `lead/ctrl_logs.pkl`); PAI: the
  baseline rollouts without a collision flag, decisions up to the first step more than 4 m from the logged path (COL1's rule).
- Decision = one `drive` call (nuPlan 2 Hz, PAI 10 Hz). Window = decisions with `now` before the first at-fault step and within 8 s of it.
- Ego box = the simulator's (nuPlan 5.176 x 2.297 m, centre 1.461 m ahead of the rear axle; PAI from the rollout spec). Object motion as
  recorded in the rollout (held at the last pose beyond the end of the record).

## A. Per-decision geometry and classes (descriptive, no line)

Per decision, served plan (8 poses at 0.5 s, current pose prepended, interpolated to 0.1 s), time-aligned against each object box:
- `clr`: minimum distance between the ego box along the plan and the object box at the same time (0 = the planned sweep intersects);
  `t_int`, `d_int`: plan time and plan arc of the first intersection; `short`: the smallest rigid lateral shift of the plan (0.1 m steps,
  up to 3 m, either side) after which the sweep clears the struck object = by how many metres the plan falls short.
- plan speed (arc / 0.5 s), curvature (yaw change / arc per segment), `alat` = max v^2 |kappa| over the plan; yaw rate demanded in the
  first 0.5 s against the yaw the ego turned in the following 0.5 s; lateral tracking error 0.5 s after the decision.

Class of a collision rollout (labels use the logged path as the lane reference):
- **(i) straight into an in-path vehicle**: at impact the ego is within 0.5 m of the logged path and the struck object is a vehicle in the
  logged corridor ahead (COL1 kinds lead stopped / slow lead; PAI classes lead stopped / slow lead), or (PAI) the object stood in the
  ego's own straight-ahead corridor at least half of the last 3 s while no plan in that time moved laterally by more than 0.5 m at the
  object's range.
- **manoeuvre**: not (i), and either a turn (logged heading change over the window >= 20 deg, or some plan in the window turns >= 20 deg in
  its 4 s) or a go-around (at the decision where the ego's offset from the logged path first exceeds 0.3 m, some object box lies in the
  ego's straight-ahead corridor within max(10 m, 4 s x speed), and the offset grows away from that object's centre).
  - **(ii)** some plan in the last 3 s before impact (excluding decisions within 0.5 s of it) has `clr` = 0 against the struck object;
  - **(iii)** none has: the planned sweeps clear, the executed one does not.
- **(iv) drift**: not (i), no manoeuvre, ego >= 0.5 m off the logged path at impact (decision 205's pattern).
- **(v) other**: the rest (the object moves into the ego's path: cut-in, crossing, from behind, while the ego is on the logged path).
The planned-sweep read (intersects / clears, `short`, lead time of the first intersecting plan) is tabulated for every class, so the
(ii) / (iii) split is also visible inside (i), (iv), (v). Class rules may be refined after looking at the clips; any change is listed.

## B. Counterfactuals (kinematic, executed path, recorded object motion)

Construction: the ego keeps its executed path; only its timing changes. Forward simulation at 0.1 s: arc position s'(t), speed
v' = min(v_orig at that arc, allowed(s')), never ahead of the original (s'(t) <= s(t)). Contact = the ego box at s'(t) overlaps an object
box at t. A collision counts as **avoided** only if there is no contact with the struck object up to the end of the rollout **and** it is
not merely deferred (the ego ends stopped short of it, or the rest of the executed path up to the original impact pose does not touch
the object's last recorded box). Deferred-only cases are reported separately (the horizon effect of decisions 178 / 203).

Arms:
1. **Lateral-acceleration cap** (non-privileged): allowed(s) = sqrt(a_lim / |kappa(s)|) from the curvature of the plan that was active when
   the original ego was at s, reached with at most 2.5 m/s^2 of braking over the plan's look-ahead. Sweep a_lim in {1.0, 1.5, 2.0, 2.5,
   3.0, 4.0} m/s^2. **Primary point 3.0** = openpilot's `MAX_LATERAL_ACCEL_NO_ROLL` (`selfdrive/controls/lib/drive_helpers.py`; in openpilot
   a clip on commanded curvature, with `MAX_LATERAL_JERK` 5 m/s^3, not a speed limiter); secondary point 1.7 = its total-acceleration budget
   in turns (`_A_TOTAL_MAX_V`, longitudinal planner). Other points are sensitivity.
2. **Lead-sweep stop** (non-privileged): at a decision, the planned sweep (ego box) is tested against a box of 4.8 x 2.0 m placed at the
   model's own lead output (`lead[:72]` -> 3 x 6 x 4, selection 0, x / y at 0, 2, 4 s interpolated in time, x from the camera to the
   lead's rear; gate `sigmoid(lead_prob[0]) >= 0.5`). If it intersects at plan arc d*, the ego brakes (at most 4 m/s^2) to stop 0.5 m
   before d*; released when the active decision no longer triggers. Source P0 (shipped weights, primary) and FT. This differs from
   FIX1's registered lead cap by using the lead's lateral position against the plan's own footprint instead of a gap along the heading.
3. **Footprint-aware stop** (privileged, **ceiling only**): the same rule with the simulator's object boxes at their recorded future
   positions.
4. **Shipped weights' plan on the same tokens** (open loop): share of window decisions at which the P0 plan's sweep intersects the struck
   object against the served plan's; a collision is "base-clear" if no P0 plan in the last 3 s intersects it.
5. **Logged human path**: COL1's `clear_on_log_path` / `clear_at_log_speed`, repeated with this lane's sweep.

Cost on N: per clean rollout, progress lost = 1 - s'(end) / s(end); a rollout "loses > 10 %" if that exceeds 0.10 and 0.5 m.

**Line B** (per domain; arms 1 at its primary point and 2 with P0; arm 3 is not eligible): avoids >= 30 % of the class (ii) + (iii)
collisions at <= 5 % of clean rollouts losing > 10 % progress = worth a closed-loop arm. Fewer than 8 class (ii) + (iii) rollouts in a
domain: descriptive only; the same two numbers are then also given over all collisions of the domain, without a line.

## C. Non-privileged signals for "my planned sweep touches something"

Label per decision: Y = 1 if the served plan's sweep intersects an object box (`clr` = 0), else 0.
**Primary read** per domain: positives = window decisions of C with Y = 1 against the struck object; negatives = decisions of N with Y = 0,
weighted to the positives' ego-speed histogram (bins 0-1, 1-3, 3-6, 6-10, > 10 m/s). AUC with a 95 % interval from a bootstrap over logs
(nuPlan: log of the scene; PAI: scene). Lead time: per collision rollout, the earliest window decision whose signal is above the
threshold of 10 % (weighted) false positives on the negatives; share of rollouts with >= 1.5 s.
Secondary read: all decisions Y = 1 against Y = 0, regardless of rollout. nuPlan APY10m10 positives are read against the P2H10 controls
and flagged (different checkpoint and input standard). Road-leaving labels are added only if the map extraction is available in time.

Signals (one scalar per decision, higher = more risk; orientation fixed here):
- `lead_sweep`: minus the minimum distance between the planned sweep and the lead box of arm B2 (20 m when gated off); P0 and FT.
- `lead_need` (reference, COL1's signal): (v_e - v_l)_+^2 / (2 max(d - 4, 0.5)) gated by p >= 0.5.
- `lead_p`: sigmoid(lead_prob[0]); `lead_near`: minus the lead's distance.
- `edge_margin`: minus the smallest distance from the ego box sides along the plan to the model's road edges (left, right), up to 4 s.
- `lane_exc`: how far the ego box along the plan reaches beyond the model's inner lane lines (probability >= 0.5), max over 4 s.
- `meta_brake`: the largest hard-brake probability in `meta`; `meta_dis`: the disengage probabilities' maximum; `desire_lc`: the largest
  lane-change entry of `desire_state` / the meta desire predictions.
- `plan_std`: lateral position spread of the served plan at 2 s and at 4 s (the plan slice's second half); P0's the same.
- `base_gap`: |lateral difference| between the served plan and the P0 plan at 2 s; `base_len`: served 4 s arc / P0 4 s arc.
- plan-only baselines (the rows a head has to beat): ego speed, `alat`, planned 4 s heading change.
- `combo`: logistic regression on all of the above, out-of-fold by log (5 folds).
- Privileged reference rows: the same sweep against the simulator's boxes frozen at the decision time (no future motion).

**Line C** (per domain, primary read): a signal is **usable** if AUC >= 0.75 and the lower bound > 0.65. No multiplicity correction over
the listed signals: a pass within 0.03 of either threshold is called a candidate only. Signals that need heads outside the stored replays
(lane lines, road edges, meta, plan spread) are read on nuPlan from a new replay through the pool; on PAI only if a Tokyo card is free
(both are held by lane FIX1 at the time of writing), otherwise the PAI read is the subset stored by COL1's replay (lead, plans) and says so.

## Reading

- Class shares in A are descriptive. D's conclusion (clearance estimation (ii) / execution (iii) / drift (iv)) follows the shares.
- B's line decides whether a serving-side arm is forwarded to lane FIX1 with its exact definition; C's line decides whether a
  non-privileged trigger exists for it. If neither passes, the output is the training-side label (swept-footprint intersection of the
  adapter's own plan with logged agents) with C's privileged and non-privileged AUCs as the learnability estimate; nothing is trained.

## Limits known in advance

- Counterfactuals are open loop: the plans are the logged ones, the model does not see its changed state, traffic is replayed.
- nuPlan scenes are 5 s with about 10 decisions; class counts are small (22 + 24 rollouts in 12 + ~14 scenes; 12 on PAI).
- The lead head reports one vehicle (three selections are the same lead at 0 / 2 / 4 s), with the near-range bias of decision 157.
