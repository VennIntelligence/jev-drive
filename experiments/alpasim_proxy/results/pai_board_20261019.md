## Feasibility: garage model_0014 on local PAI 281 (not run)

Decision 387 holds: there is no local garage PAI read (box `runs/` has only `alpasim_proxy/setup_garage`; no PAI chunk job has a garage
tag). What exists and what is missing:

- **The model accepts the PAI rig.** `model_0014` was trained on nuPlan + Physical AI AV (`ltf_mixed_nuplan_physical_ai_av_trainval`,
  checkpoint README); `sensor_rig_2.yaml` / `sensor_rig_3.yaml` next to the checkpoint on the box are the PAI train / val rigs (ftheta
  `camera_cross_left_120fov`, `camera_front_wide_120fov`, `camera_cross_right_120fov`). All three are in the PAI preset's `6cam_1080`.
  The host's own `014-pai-0014-v1` (PCS 1819, mean 0.436) is this checkpoint on PAI, so a local read has a private anchor.
- **Garage repo (v0.1.1 = upstream main 288d34c, cloned on the box):** one track-agnostic `lib/alpasim/tools/submission.Dockerfile`
  (rig chosen by `SENSOR_RIG_FILE` build arg) and `scripts/evaluation/alpasim/latent_transfuser.sh`, whose default (no `+cameras=nuplan_3cam`)
  is the PAI track; `configs/driver/garage_transfuser.yaml` uses the three 120fov cameras. No separate PAI Dockerfile is needed.
- **Our side, three small gaps:** (1) `drivers/garage.sh` hardcodes `sensor_rig_0.yaml` (nuPlan): needs a rig env (PAI: `sensor_rig_2.yaml`;
  which of 2 / 3 the host used is unknown, a 2-scene check of both is cheap). (2) `pai_native.sh` hardcodes driver `pai` in its `run.sh`
  call and `pai_box.sh` passes SH30 env: needs a `DRV` switch. (3) `ALPASIM_FAST=1` (`lib/sim_fast.py`) needs one module-level `CAM`;
  garage reads three cameras, so either run `ALPASIM_FAST=0` (official set) or extend `sim_fast` to a camera list.
  Env `envs/garage` and the checkpoint are already on the box; all 441 curated-val PAI scenes are on the box disk.
- **Cost of one PAI 281 read** (pool, `pai_box.sh`, CONC 4, chunk lists as the g10 read): 8 chunk jobs. With `ALPASIM_FAST=0`
  (pool `--vram 28 --cpu 6 --ram 40`): about 85 s wall per scene per stack (results/pai_fast.md, g10 official), so about 6.6
  stack-hours, about 55 min wall if 8 stacks run at once (about 3 stacks fit a card). With a 3-camera fast mode: video off is two
  thirds of the 2.5x gain, so expect about 45-55 s per scene, about 3.5-4.3 stack-hours, 30-35 min wall. Garage inference (ResNet-34,
  1024 x 256) is not the bottleneck; the renderer is. Plus about 1 h of code (the three gaps) and a 2-scene smoke per rig.
