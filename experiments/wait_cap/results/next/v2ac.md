**Bottom line.** All five prereg lines against V2 are met on this seed: no cost shows up beyond one-seed noise. The evidence that the noise removes V2's creep is thin: progress and distance
driven rise a little (held progress_rel +0.013, distance / log +0.023), but the scenes that creep hard (under 30 % of the log's distance) do not change in number, and the
hard no-launch scenes stay low. A second seed is the next step; this is not a submission candidate.

## Question and arm

Does adding history-speed noise to V2's recipe remove V2's creep (the policy's own low speed history keeps it slow) without new costs?

**V2AC-L18-s0** = V2's command (`nx.sh train rtbo=RTB1AR-F-s0`: RSG-L18 recipe, 2 500 steps, lr x 0.1, old pool on1_L18RB0, seed 0, `--init RTB1AR-F-s0 --allow-init`) plus
`--ego-scale-speed 0.5 --ego-scale-max 0.2`: on half of the imitation rows the fed speed history is rescaled by s ~ U[0.8, 1.2] (speeds x s, accelerations x s^2, history poses
x s), the target stays the log. The expanded command differs from V2's in the tag and these two flags only. It trained eager (pool job env `JEV_RM_EAGER=1`, as V2 did;
`rm_train` compiles by default since 20d5ef00) at code revision 6e1c38f2.

Training record: 2 500 steps in 457 s, DONE dev ADE 0.5486 (base RTB1AR-F-s0 0.5519, V2 0.5445, RSG 0.5284). The logged noise matches the design (`loss/scale/abs` 0.100 = E|s-1|
for U[0.8, 1.2]; `loss/scale/share` 0.35 of all batch rows = half of the non-anchor, non-pool rows). `on_hash_60` and `on_hash_2500` equal V2's and RSG's, so the pool draws are
identical: only the history noise differs.

References are the stored V2 = RTB1ARO-L18-s0 and RSG-L18-s0 reads with the same configs (held 460 @18, public 1491 @10, static public scene, PAI stop17 subset). Paired columns use
the session-clustered CI of `wt.xpair`; every arm has one training seed and one read; V2AC has no PAI 281 read. The whole chain took 27 min of wall clock.

## Against the lines

By the registered reading V2AC passes: held zeros 39 (V2 39; the line is <= 41), held progress_rel 0.830 against V2's 0.817, public paired mean against V2 +0.001 with the CI lower bound at
-0.0036 (line -0.005), public rear-ended scenes 15 against 17, and the static scene stops at 0.84 m without contact. The margins are thin and all inside what one seed can produce: line 3 clears by
0.0014, line 2 by 0.013, and the held paired mean is +0.009 [-0.006, 0.035]. The direct creep counts do not move (scenes under 30 % of the log: 8 held and 17 public for both V2 and V2AC), the
class N scene-reads still average 0.69 against RSG's 0.79 (V2 0.65), and the lateral zeros are untouched. What does move is small and one-directional: distance relative to the log +0.023 held
and +0.006 public, held at-fault 7 to 4, completed held turns 16 to 19 of 24, public rear-ended 17 to 15, and 9 scene-reads gain 0.1 or more with none losing that much. The cost-side wobbles are one
more stop17 zero (16 of 17 against V2's 15; not a line, and the static scene still stops), public front collisions 5 against 3, and the two borderline under-1 m scenes. Reading: no measurable cost, a
possible small gain in progress, no removal of the hard creep; the next step is the second seed (and PAI 281), not a decision.

## Caveats

- One training seed per arm. The CIs are over scenes within that seed; they do not cover seed-to-seed spread, and V2's second base seed was cancelled, so there is no V2 reseed to compare with.
  The only seed pair on hand is RSG-L18-s1 against s0: held zeros 34 / 34, held progress_rel 0.788 / 0.788, public paired +0.001 [-0.003, 0.005], and 4 up / 1 down scene-reads on this set.
- The 28 named scenes came from the zero- and low-score videos of arm a (RTB1O) shown next to RSG, and the 12 no-launch scenes from arm a's public read, so V2, a close relative of a, sits below
  its own average on the set; a retrained V2 would tend to drift up there. 9 up / 0 down is suggestive, not evidence for the mechanism.
- The by-class mean table, the distance / log table (`nx_regress.py dist`) and the RSG seed table were added after the first read of the results; they are descriptive. The five lines and the
  status rule were fixed before any V2AC output and were not changed.
- Held and public are single reads of 9.5 s and 5.5 s windows; the held 460 scenes are a subset of the public 1491.

## Reuse

