**Question.** Is the longitudinal consequence of the student's own plan, (A) reaching a stopped or slow vehicle ahead in its lane, (B) passing
a mandatory stop point while the stop is mandatory, (C) entering a > 45 deg turn faster than the logged driver, readable from what the
driver has at inference time, with a read directly comparable to BODY1 arm S0's lateral one (decision 223)?

**Conclusion.** Readable, mostly without perception: on hold logs (own plan + its arc re-timings) A reaches 0.987 [0.982, 0.991] (PARTIAL,
+0.033 over a blind floor of 0.953, carried equally by vision tokens and openpilot's own lead outputs, in launches), B 0.969 [0.949, 0.984]
(READABLE, +0.056), C 0.991 (NOT READABLE by the gain clause because the plan's own speed and curvature already give 0.97-0.98); the
longitudinal blind floors (0.91-0.98) sit far above the lateral one (0.83), so the registered gain separates "vision adds" from "already
predictable" (decision 256).
