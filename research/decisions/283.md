## Setting

- **R-A**: the lane sequence the logged path follows (map successor links, filled connectors, lane-change cuts). Centre and both
  boundaries at 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 30 m of route arc ahead of the camera's projection, in the rear-axle frame. It is defined on
  ≥ 0.94 of turn decisions.
- **R-B**: a 48 × 48 grid at 0.5 m, 0-24 m ahead, with classes off-road / drivable / oncoming; junction lanes are always drivable.
- The probe is HEAD1 (`head1_train.build`) on the frozen 8-slot tokens plus ego, 8 000 steps, 2 seeds averaged. It is fit on
  P = J_nav (navtrain) ∪ J_on (AlpaSim train split on-policy rows), minus val: 120 115 rows.
- Read sets: navtest-16 (real frames, 16 held drive logs), held-452 (reconstructed frames, YS-L18-s1), zero-98 (the off-road zero windows).
- Buckets: by route distance to the junction; turn decisions have turn40 ≥ 20°. CIs are cluster bootstraps, B 2000.

## Verdict and meaning

By the registered line: **go to step 1**. Three of the four Z-0 conditions fail; only the navtest-16 off-road ratio passes. On the fair
ruler the junction gap is mostly real:
- The blind floor is 1.5-2.1 m near junctions on real frames, against 0.73-0.99 m for the tokens. The old ruler's 0.29 m token-over-blind
  gap was label difficulty.
- The tokens still double their error near junctions (0.99 vs 0.46 m at 20 m).
- Reconstructed frames are clearly worse than real ones on the new ruler (1.13 vs 0.73 at 15 m; off-road IoU in junction 0.69 vs 0.78).
  On the old ruler the two looked equal.

a method). The step-1 G1 bar is therefore the 0.10 m floor.
