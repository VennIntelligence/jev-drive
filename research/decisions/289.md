# 289. ALPASIM-HUMAN: a person drives the PAI track by phone wheel at real time and scores 0.275 on the 10 stage-1 scenes, above P2H10 (0.154) and the organisers' mean (0.198), below alpamayo1 (0.438); every zero is lateral (corridor or offroad), none a collision. The human's own account: the failures are interface and speed-into-turn, not road judgement. Weak evidence, not an exam-fairness verdict.

- Date 2026-10-13 (Tokyo, card 0). Experiment `experiments/alpasim_human/` (README, per-scene tables in `results/`). Tooling:
  `lib/human_driver.py` (gRPC driver whose plan is a kinematic-bicycle rollout of the phone's steer and pedals, the same 41-pose,
  4 s trajectory the model drivers return; the official MPC tracks it), viewer with the route projected on the road, click-to-start,
  sim paced to wall time. Front camera only, no eval video (`HUMAN_MODE=fast`); official 6-camera settings run at 1.7-1.9 wall s per
  sim s on one RTX 3090 and are kept behind `HUMAN_MODE=official`. Numbers are not leaderboard-comparable.
- Two sessions, one person, one attempt per scene, controls still being tuned: unpaced (2x fast-forward, by accident) 0.270;
  paced (1.00 wall s per sim s, 0 stale phone frames) 0.275: 2 pass, 1 slow (0.752), 7 zeros = 4 `left_corridor_laterally` +
  3 `offroad`. Same scenes: P2H10-F-s0 0.154, alpamayo1 0.438, organisers' 8-reference mean 0.198. Per scene the human and the
  model disagree a lot (724: human 1.0 / model 0; f33: both 1.0; 257: human offroad after 4 m).
- The user's reading, recorded as the interpretation of this entry: the phone wheel gives no body or pedal feel and cannot pause;
  speed was not shed before turns, so at the turn the road was unreadable and the car could not be turned; the scenes driven were
  mostly straight and real turns would all have failed. "I am a model without ego state": the speed panel exists but is not felt.
  So 0.275 is a lower bound for a human and a control-familiarity number, not a judgement number. It is not evidence that the
  corridor / offroad test is unreasonable (the earlier reading in chat was withdrawn).
- What it does support: the human fails where the model fails and by the same mechanism, speed into the turn then lateral
  departure (decision 285's speed ratchet / turn-approach line gets an independent witness); the corridor is a spatial test
  with no speed term, so entering fast is the whole failure.
- Evidence that would change it: a slowed-down (0.5x) session that separates reaction time from judgement; a physical USB wheel
  and pedals; the same person after practice; turn-heavy scenes. The nuPlan launcher exists but was never run.
- Loading: 154 of 496 s per 10-scene session; most of it the renderer's PTX JIT on the first scene of each artifact revision,
  now cached on the host (`HUMAN_JIT_CACHE=1`, about 54 s saved per launch); 4-5 s between scenes is AlpaSim itself.
